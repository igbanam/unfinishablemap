## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found
- **Internal contradiction, Relation to Site Perspective (Dualism).** The paragraph said "Dualism explains both why the Map escapes the threat from within (phenomenal character is narrow and irreducible)…". The hybrid section says the Map "concedes the threat from within for wide content as such". Narrowness comes from phenomenal internalism, the hybrid's internalist half. Dualism does not supply it, and irreducibility does no work against WITHIN. **Fix** (30 → 29 words): "Dualism explains why program explanation is unavailable to the Map (consciousness has no physical realizer); the hybrid view, not dualism, puts the content clause beyond the threat from within."

### Medium Issues Found
- **Unsupported universal (lead and parity).** "every physicalist payment runs through physical realization" is false on the page's own analysis. The counterfactual and explanatory-practice standards need no realizer; they fail only because they cannot tell content from an epiphenomenal quale. The three payments the parity section names are exactly what SEP §6.4 calls inheritance solutions: program explanation, Yablo's determinables and the subset strategy. **Fix**: in the lead, "every physicalist payment runs" → "physicalism's inheritance solutions run"; in parity, "The physicalist payments run" → "Physicalism's inheritance solutions run".
- **"No new debt" sat uneasily with the costs paragraph.** The costs paragraph admits an unestablished success condition: the narrow components must carry the evidential relations. That condition is epistemic, not causal. **Fix**: "no new debt" → "no new causal debt" (lead), and "no second debt" → "no second causal debt" (What it buys).
- **J&P 1990 p. 201 paraphrase.** "locate the difference" overstated the text's "a major difference". The paraphrase also dropped "historical" from "certain environmental and historical factors". **Fix**: "a major difference"; "(with environment and history for broad roles)".
- **The standards section never said which standard the Map picks.** The lead says the reply "holds only relative to a chosen standard". The six bullets end with "has to say which", but the Map's choice (inclusion, L84 "The asymmetry rests on inclusion") appeared only two sections later. **Fix**: `…has to say which; the [[#hybrid-route|Map's answer]] is inclusion.` (+5)
