## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **Internal contradiction: the Map was filed with the egalitarians while keeping a centring fact** (pessimistic 10-14 Issue 1, confirmed). The lede said the quadrilemma *forces* the fragmentalist exit and then that the Map "keeps" the fact simpliciter that the egalitarian form of that exit removes. "Other minds — calibration" said the Map "on parity already stands with the egalitarians against Hare and Merlo". Merlo 2021 keeps parity at the objective level too ("All minds are 'on a par'", verified verbatim), so holding every subject conscious does not separate the camps; a fact simpliciter about which subject is this one is the centring Builes fn 46 calls being "special simpliciter". **Resolution**: the lede now says the egalitarian form "meets parity and multiplicity by construction, which the Map's posit has not shown it can do" (replacing the criterion-less "handles other minds at least as well"). It also says that keeping the fact makes the posit Merlo-shaped and, inside the fragmentalist exit, a privileged fragment that drifts back towards Hare, and that the Map holds the fact "pending a choice". The calibration paragraph locates the posit on the inegalitarian side of Merlo's question, has it inherit Merlo's "fine line" concession, and names the fork: either a privileged fragment (which `egocentric-presentism` says "collapses back towards Hare") or a triad not yet made consistent. RSP No Many Worlds: "declines it" became "has not taken it", with one sentence saying the fact has no consistent home short of a privileged fragment. The sibling pages (`vertiginous-question` L107, `egocentric-presentism` L81, `apex/one-world-wager` L48) now agree with this page; none was touched.
2. **Misdescribed source principle (new; not in either prior review).** L40 glossed Merlo's Appearance/Reality Principle as making appearances "a guide to how things are absent defeaters". Merlo's (ARP 3) reads: "If J is a judgment concerning the phenomenal characters of one's own experiences and it is not epistemically based on other beliefs, then if J is justified it is also correct". He also states explicitly that the issue is *not* defeat ("the suggestion is not that … I acquire reasons which defeat my immediate justification"). The gloss turned a no-gap principle into a defeasible one. **Resolution**: "denies any appearance/reality gap for phenomenal character: an immediate, justified judgment about one's own experiences is also correct" (+8).
3. **Spliced quotation** (pessimistic 10-14 Unsupported row, confirmed). Kinkaid abstract: the raw text reads "'inegalitarian' forms of first-person realism are, I argue, vulnerable …"; the article had dropped ", I argue," and the scare quotes. **Restored.**

A corpus grep of `egalitarian` across topics/concepts/apex/voids/positions/tenets (body hits listed individually) found no page that equates egalitarianism with denying first-person realism; `egocentric-presentism` L81 already calls Fine and Lipman egalitarian. **Resolution**: "One terminological point matters, because two different views go by 'egalitarian'"; the Terminology entry now states the distinction to keep, not a correction.
