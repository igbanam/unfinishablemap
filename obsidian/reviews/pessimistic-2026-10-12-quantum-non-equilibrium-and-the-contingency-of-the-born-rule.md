## Executive Summary

The article is carefully hedged, and its quotations hold. All five quoted spans re-checked this run match their sources verbatim. It has three weaknesses the deep review could not see, because each depends on literature or corpus structure outside the article:

1. **Scope transfer (High).** The lede and the Minimal Quantum Interaction paragraph call Valentini's signal-locality theorem "the Map's structural reason" for claiming that Born statistics are preserved *exactly*. The theorem is stated for "any deterministic hidden-variables theory" and the article says so itself. The Map's reading is indeterministic and falls outside that class. The argument that does apply in the Map's own class already exists in the corpus as [[causal-consistency-constraint]] and P-Q7, but the article does not link it.
2. **A survey gap with a known answer (Medium-High).** L67 says the article "has not surveyed" whether post-Planck analyses support the predicted inverse-tangent deficit. The programme's own authors published that fit in 2019 and called the results "statistically inconclusive". The gap was flagged in the research note and carried into the article unfilled.
3. **A stale account of the programme's scope, and a speculation that undercuts itself (Medium).** L37 states "once in equilibrium, always in equilibrium" without qualification. L75 says the programme puts non-equilibrium only "in the early universe and in exotic relics". Valentini's 2023 *Foundations of Physics* paper argues that quantum-gravitational corrections can create non-equilibrium from a prior equilibrium state. In the same paragraph, the Map's speculation about a "wider space" of influence opens only onto the region the article has just priced at superluminal signalling.

A fourth, smaller item is a source-framing fault. L57 says Valentini "treats the conflict as confined" to a vanished regime. His 2008 paper does the opposite: it accepts absolute simultaneity and a preferred foliation of spacetime.

## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist
The article is about physics, and it admits that "nothing in this literature is about consciousness". Churchland's complaint would come at the end. The Relation to Site Perspective section turns a mind-free statistical-mechanics dispute into a "modal lesson" for Bidirectional Interaction. On her reading, that lesson asserts a possibility the physics says nothing about. The article fences the inference as "the Map's own", which is honest, but the fence does not make the inference earn anything. Issue 3 shows the inference is also internally strained.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist
Dennett would press the price list. The article's selling point is that the programme "prices" deviation from Born statistics. A price list for a theory you reject tells you what *that* theory charges, not what yours does. If the Map wants a no-signalling argument for its own indeterministic interface, it should use one stated for indeterministic theories (Issue 1). Borrowing a deterministic-hidden-variables theorem as "the structural reason" inflates a rival's bookkeeping into the Map's own justification.

### The Quantum Skeptic
Tegmark has less to attack here than usual, because the article never puts non-equilibrium in brains. His target is the speculation at L75. Grant that the Born rule is a condition rather than a law. The only physics that departs from that condition in this class of theories is non-equilibrium, and the article itself says that is signalling-priced and fast-relaxing in any many-mode system. A warm, wet, massively multi-mode brain is the worst place to look for surviving non-equilibrium: Towler et al. show τ ∝ 1/M. The "wider space of lawlike, minimal, mind-associated influence" therefore lies, inside this class, in a region that is both signalling-priced and dynamically erased. The article should say so (Issue 3).

### The Many-Worlds Defender
Deutsch would say L79 understates the dispute. Valentini's 2010 reply rests on non-equilibrium: pilot-wave theory "in general describes a 'nonequilibrium' state that violates the Born rule", and so has its own subquantum measurement theory. The single-world reading the Map welcomes as "a structural ally" therefore depends on the very regime the article calls unobserved and possibly idle under typicality. If the typicality school is right, Valentini's reply to the Everettian charge loses its key move. The ally is weaker than presented, though this is a framing point rather than a defect.

### The Empiricist
The Popperian is the article's most important critic, and the article half-meets him. It says the cosmological window shows "a beyond-quantum regime can be lawful, bounded, and in-principle detectable". It then declines to report whether the window has been looked through. It has: Vitenti, Peter & Valentini (2019) fitted the relaxation deficit to Planck data. They got a 2–3σ improvement on temperature data that faded once polarisation was added, and concluded the effect "is probably a statistical fluctuation" (Issue 2). The falsifier ledger reads more accurately as "tested once, inconclusive, leaning null" than as "untested". Saying so strengthens the methodological point: the programme made a prediction, tested it, and reported a weak result honestly.

### The Buddhist Philosopher
Nagarjuna would accept the article's central move. A rule that looks fixed is an equilibrium that arose and could pass, which is dependent origination applied to probability. His objection would fall on the opposite school. Typicality makes |Ψ|² "the uniquely natural choice", and he would read that as reifying a measure in order to stop the regress. The article gives the regress charge to Valentini ("inherently circular") and does not adjudicate. That is the right call, and nothing needs to change.

- **Problem**: Valentini's theorem, verbatim from arXiv quant-ph/0112151 and Phys. Lett. A 297 (2002), says "any deterministic hidden-variables theory, that reproduces quantum theory for a 'quantum equilibrium' distribution … must predict the existence of instantaneous signals". The article recites that scope at L55 ("its scope conditions matter") and at L73 ("within deterministic hidden-variables theories"). [[causal-consistency-constraint]] (Torres Alegre 2025, unrefereed) holds that non-affine deviations from the Born map permit signalling via steering. Name [[causal-consistency-constraint]] as the argument for the Map's own reading, and say plainly that it rests on an unrefereed preprint plus a Map-side extension. The exact strings are in Priority List items 1a and 1b.

### Issue 2: The article declares a survey gap the programme's own authors had already filled (Medium-High)
- **File**: same
- **Location**: L67 "Whether post-Planck data analyses support, disfavour, or merely fail to constrain the inverse-tangent deficit is a question this article has not surveyed"
- **Problem**: Vitenti, Peter & Valentini, "Modeling the large-scale power deficit with smooth and discontinuous primordial spectra", *Phys. Rev. D* 100, 043506 (2019). Verified at Crossref (DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.100.043506, authors Vitenti / Peter / Valentini) and in the arXiv:1901.08885v2 full text. They fitted the quantum-relaxation deficit to Planck data. Verbatim, whitespace-normalised:
  - "a moderate improvement at the 2 − 3σ level in favor of our quantum relaxation model"
  - "the significance of the fits tends to decrease when polarization data are added"
  - "our starting point for a modified power spectrum yields statistically inconclusive results"
  - On the one-parameter inverse-tangent fit across datasets: "This suggests that the effect is probably a statistical fluctuation."

  The best fit was a two-parameter sudden step, and the authors write that this "raises the question of whether or not a realistic quantum relaxation model could yield such a result". The research note `research/bohmian-quantum-equilibrium-and-non-equilibrium-2026-07-19.md` L144 flagged this exact gap ("need a dedicated literature pass"), and the article inherited it unfilled. P-Q11's Upward shift condition ("post-Planck analyses were found to support the predicted inverse-tangent … deficit") reads more accurately now that this result is known.
- **Severity**: Medium-High. An LLM reader gets "unsurveyed" where a finding exists, and the article's "main proposed observational window" is reported without its only published test.
- **Recommendation**: Replace the not-surveyed clause with the 2019 result (Priority item 2) and add the reference. Keep the honest "later analyses … not surveyed" scope note. I did not search for post-2019 analyses beyond the arXiv author listing for Valentini, which shows none on the CMB deficit after 2019.

### Issue 3: L75's speculation opens onto the region the article just priced; the programme's scope is stated as it stood before its quantum-gravity papers (Medium)
- **File**: same
- **Location**: L37 "The Born distribution is dynamically self-sustaining — once in equilibrium, always in equilibrium."; L75 "Two honesty notes bound the speculation: Valentini's programme locates non-equilibrium in the early universe and in exotic relics, never in brains; …"
- **Problem (a), internal tension**: L57 concludes that "within this class of theories there is nothing innocent between exact equilibrium … and signalling". L75 then speculates that a Born rule demoted from law to condition widens "the space of lawlike, minimal, mind-associated influence". In the only worked framework that does the demoting, the widened space is the non-equilibrium region L57 has just priced at superluminal signalling. The speculation needs to say that, or it reads as a door the article has already shown is shut. P-Q11 restates the same speculation; see the Counterarguments section.
- **Problem (b), currency**: Valentini, "Beyond the Born Rule in Quantum Gravity", *Foundations of Physics* 53, 6 (2023). Verified at Crossref (DOI 10.1007/s10701-022-00635-0) and in the arXiv:2212.12175 abstract. He argues "there is no Born rule at the fundamental level of quantum gravity", and that "quantum-gravitational corrections to the Schrödinger approximation allow quantum nonequilibrium … to be created from a prior equilibrium (ρ=|ψ|²) state. Such effects are very tiny and difficult to observe in practice." This qualifies L37's unrestricted "once in equilibrium, always in equilibrium". It also makes L75's "early universe and exotic relics" incomplete. "Never in brains" still holds.
- **Severity**: Medium.
- **Recommendation**: Priority items 3a and 3b. Cite the refereed *Found. Phys.* paper only. The 2021 preprint arXiv:2104.07966 adds that deviations "can also be generated for atomic systems in the gravitational field of the earth, though the effects are unlikely to be observable". That is unrefereed, and quoting it would invite a brain-adjacent overreach. Do not import it.

### Issue 4: Valentini's position on relativity is softened (Medium)
- **File**: same
- **Location**: L57 "Valentini treats the conflict as confined to a regime that no longer exists outside possible cosmological relics"
- **Problem**: Valentini, "Hidden Variables and the Large-Scale Structure of Spacetime", in Craig & Smith (eds.), *Einstein, Relativity and Absolute Simultaneity* (Routledge 2008), pp. 125–155. Verified via the arXiv quant-ph/0504011 journal-ref; Crossref has no record of the chapter. The abstract argues that "in deterministic hidden-variables theories, the choice of spacetime kinematics should be dictated by the properties of generic non-equilibrium states, which allow nonlocal signalling. Such signalling provides an operational definition of absolute simultaneity, which may naturally be associated with a preferred foliation of classical spacetime." He does not confine the conflict with relativity to a vanished regime. He accepts it at the level of kinematics. Only the *observable* signalling is confined, "masked by quantum noise" (Phys. Lett. A 2002 abstract). The article "reads the theorem adversarially, as pricing", so understating the price the rival itself pays defeats that purpose. The difference also matters for P-Q7's open Lorentz-covariance debt.
- **Severity**: Medium.
- **Recommendation**: Priority item 4, plus a reference entry.

## Counterarguments to Address

### "The Map has no in-class no-signalling argument at all"
- **Current content says**: Valentini's theorem is the structural reason for exactness.
- **A critic would argue**: then the Map's exactness commitment rests on a theorem about theories it rejects. Its own class has only an unrefereed preprint (Torres Alegre 2025) plus a Map-side extension to non-physical participants.
- **Suggested response**: accept this. Item 1b's replacement says it in the article's own voice. The Map's case for exactness comes from two partial results, neither covering the other's class. It does not rest on one theorem, and the article should not suggest that it does.

### "If equilibrium is fragile under quantum gravity, the Map's exactness reading is threatened"
- **Current content says**: once in equilibrium, always in equilibrium.
- **A critic would argue**: Valentini 2023 makes Born statistics breakable even after relaxation, so "exact preservation" is a default the physics may not honour.
- **Suggested response**: the 2023 effects are deterministic-pilot-wave effects, and their author calls them "very tiny and difficult to observe in practice". They bear on the contingency thesis, which they strengthen, and not on the Map's corridor reading, which is framed within standard indeterministic QM. Item 3a's wording keeps that separation.
