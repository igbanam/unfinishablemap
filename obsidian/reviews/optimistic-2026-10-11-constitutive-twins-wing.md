## Executive Summary

These are two of the most carefully calibrated entries in the register. P-MA1 books its own standing as "unrefuted and untested, the weakest of the falsifier states", adding "unrefuted is not survived" (L49). The 09-21 audit found it is the register's only entry graded D with direct discriminability, and called it the model case for that vocabulary. P-TU1 separates what the Map holds (the constitutive reading) from what the evidence delivers ("constrains without establishing"). It calls the two "one position read at two grains" (L46). It also names the Würzburg–Titchener lesson as the reason no first-personal evidence can raise its grade (L49). Both entries run the same foundational-dependency test and conclude that retirement would narrow Tenet 1's claimed domain without touching its rationale. Both keep themselves out of the arguments-for-dualism file, which is a self-limiting domain, for the same stated reason.

Read side by side, the twins appear to disagree in three places. Each disagreement resolves, and **neither entry states the resolution** ([The Convergence](#the-convergence)):

1. **Different relata.** P-TU1's constitutive horn (PCT) says phenomenal character constitutes *content*. P-MA1 says the autonoetic mode constitutes the *kind* of memory while content is held fixed, using Klein's manner/content separation (L50). The two fit together only if felt pastness is the phenomenology of an attitude rather than of a content. Horgan & Tienson (2002), already cited on four Map pages, draw exactly that line: "phenomenology of intentional content" versus "phenomenology of attitude type". The Map has never used the second half of that distinction. Fernández's (2019) intentionalist reading of pastness falls on the content side, so it is the reading that would separate the twins.
2. **Different kind-claims, opposite falsifier shapes.** P-TU1 says "the conservative needs a zero, not a gradient" (L46). P-MA1's falsifier *is* a gradient: felt pastness "fading by degrees within a single memory" (L52). The two are consistent. P-TU1's liberal claim is existential (some phenomenal character versus none), and only a zero threatens it. P-MA1's claim is that two *positive* modes, noetic and autonoetic, differ in kind, and a gradient between them threatens that. A reader comparing the entries, which P-MA1's "same structure" invites, will see a contradiction unless that principle is written down.
3. **The same case is support on one side and a falsifier on the other.** In memory, competence without phenomenology (SDAM: autobiographical content intact, re-experiencing absent) is read as support. In thought, competence without phenomenology is the falsifier. The resolution is in the anoetic page's own wording: SDAM adults "hold autobiographical content in the noetic mode" (L96). SDAM therefore lacks autonoesis but keeps the noetic phenomenology P-TU1 protects, so it is no zero for P-TU1.

Three smaller gains are ready, and each has been checked:

- **A stale sibling of the project's own worked exhibit.** `project/coherence-inflation-countermeasures` (L646) uses `phenomenology-of-memory-and-the-self` to show the falsifier-absorption audit at work. That page strikes two candidates, "a successful computational account of mental time travel, and a functionalist account of semanticisation", as "decorative". The anoetic page, the source of the original list, still names both as challenges (L136, L139) and states that the two articles "face the standing challenges jointly" (L141). The parent's 2026-08-04 refine (`5208d41590`) did not reach it. `episodic-memory` (L166) and `phenomenology-of-intellectual-life` (L226) have both since run the audit. Among the challenge lists in the memory wing, the anoetic page is the only one not yet audited.
- **An unsourced reliability claim against the corpus's own void.** `cognitive-phenomenology` L127 says that if the felt difference in mathematical understanding "were confabulation, it wouldn't predict cognitive success so reliably". No source is given. `voids/noetic-feelings-void` L106 records Rozenblit & Keil's illusion of explanatory depth, where felt understanding outruns explanatory knowledge. The inference also fails as stated: a cue-based feeling can predict success without being phenomenal.
- **A testable form of P-TU1's zero case.** Semantic satiation is already in the corpus (`recognition-void`, `memory-anomalies`) but not in the thought wing. Repetition can drive the felt meaning of a word toward zero, and Tian & Huber (2010) measured competence in the same paradigm. This is a harvester subject (M1).

## The Convergence

### One structure, two relata

P-MA1's dependency line calls P-TU1 "the same constitutive-versus-accompaniment structure registered for thought" (L50). The structure matches. The relata do not:

| | P-TU1 (thought) | P-MA1 (memory) |
|---|---|---|
| What phenomenal character constitutes | **content**: PCT is "exclusively about phenomenal character constituting intentional content" (PCT L70) | **the kind** of memory: "constitutive of the *kind* of memory you have" (`episodic-memory` L118) |
| What is held fixed | — | content: "the same proposition be held autonoetically or noetically" (P-MA1 L50, Klein 2016) |
| Kind-claim | existential: some phenomenal character versus none | between-types: two positive modes differ in kind |
| Falsifier shape | a **zero**: "genuine understanding, with reliable transfer and error-detection, and no reportable phenomenal character" (L49) | a **gradient**: felt pastness "fading by degrees within a single memory" with access controlled (L52) |
| Decomposition test | phenomenal-contrast cases reduced "entirely to subtle sensory or evaluative differences" (L49) | felt pastness "decomposed into separately manipulable cues with no residual" (L52) |
| Construct worry | Fürst 2017: judgements "regimented by the view initially held" (L47) | De Brigard's construct-validity limb (L52) |
| Grade · discriminability | C · indirect | D · direct |

The rows that match (decomposition test, construct worry) show the same discipline applied twice. The rows that differ are each correct for their own claim, and in each case the reason is unstated.

**What makes them compose.** PCT is cited to the phenomenal-intentionality programme, "whose sharpest statement is Horgan and Tienson's" (PCT L64). The same paper has a second half the Map has never used. It distinguishes "the phenomenology of intentional content" from "the phenomenology of attitude type": "There is a phenomenological difference between wondering whether rabbits have tails on one hand and thinking that rabbits have tails on the other. This aspect is the phenomenology of attitude type." (Both terms were grepped twice each in the consc.net-hosted text marked "To appear in (Chalmers, ed.) *Philosophy of Mind: Classical and Contemporary Readings* (OUP, 2002)", which is PCT's reference 7. The anthology pagination was not checked.) A grep of `topics/ concepts/ positions/` and the memory wing for "attitude type", "attitude-type" or "phenomenology of attitude" returns 0. The control word "attitud" finds `intentionality` L185 and `agency-and-will` L103, both on unrelated subjects.

If remembering and merely knowing are two attitudes toward one content, the twins stack. PCT supplies the floor: the content is constituted by its content phenomenology, which the noetic and autonoetic holdings share. P-MA1 supplies the upper storey: the autonoetic attitude phenomenology constitutes the kind. PCT already anticipates this. Its contrast with identity names "the same content under different experiential modes" as a counterexample "the constitution reading absorbs" (L56). It never says how. The attitude/content split is the answer, and remembering versus knowing is the Map's clearest example. `semantic-memory` L83–L87 already places PCT under noetic memory, so the floor is in print.

**The reading that would split them.** `phenomenology-of-memory-and-the-self` L105 lists Fernández's (2019) intentionalist reading as a deflationary rival: "the memory represents itself as caused by a past experience". That puts pastness in *content*. On P-TU1's constitutive horn, a content is constituted by its phenomenology, so the reading would not deflate pastness. It would move it into cognitive phenomenology, and the article's last sentence nearly says so: "the irreducibility claim would transfer from pastness to qualia generally". But it would cost P-MA1 the manner/content separation. Remembering and knowing would then differ in content, and the "retaining content" premise of the semanticisation wager would fail. So whether felt pastness belongs to the attitude or to the content decides whether the twins stack or diverge. The register should record that choice explicitly (priority item 2).

**Why the opposite falsifiers are both right.** P-TU1's sentence "The claim concerns *kind*, not intensity … so the conservative needs a zero, not a gradient" (L46) comes from `cognitive-phenomenology` L188 and the self-consciousness exemplar "structurally necessary in kind while scalar in degree". It is scoped to the liberal claim, which is existential. P-MA1's gradient falsifier fits a claim of a different form. Semanticisation fades one *positive* mode (autonoetic) into another (noetic, which has phenomenology of its own: the tip-of-the-tongue "feeling of imminent retrieval", anoetic L56). A smooth blend between two positive types is what dissolves a difference in kind between them. A fade in intensity within one type does no such damage. So the principle the corpus uses without stating is: **existence claims are refuted by zeros, and between-types claims by gradients**. P-MA1 could have defended itself with the thought wing's "kind, not degree" reply and did not. That is the strongest evidence of restraint in the wing (see the Hardline Empiricist section).

**SDAM resolves the remaining asymmetry.** The memory wing reads severely deficient autobiographical memory as "content/manner independence in its cleanest available form" (`semanticisation` L62). P-TU1 treats competence without phenomenology as its falsifier. The anoetic page shows these are consistent: SDAM adults "hold autobiographical content in the noetic mode" (L96). SDAM is a zero for the autonoetic attitude and not for content phenomenology. The register can say this in one clause (priority item 2), which saves a later reviewer from finding a false contradiction.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Both entries widen the hard problem's domain in a careful way. P-TU1 widens the explanatory gap "from sensation to thought" and P-MA1 "from sensation to the temporal case". Each states the limit at once. Each depends on P-D1, so each sharpens the explanandum "without adding an independent confirmation" (P-MA1 L50, P-TU1 L47). The memory wing's bounded relocation reply is the best single argument in the wing. It concedes that "an argument shielded that thoroughly earns nothing distinctive from memory science, resting on the general hard-problem premise and nothing more" (`phenomenology-of-memory-and-the-self` L119), and then names the two results that do survive. Chalmers would recognise the move: a dualist argument that names which empirical results it cannot absorb.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

The wing keeps its distance from the quantum interface, and that is a strength. `episodic-memory` L136–L138 offers microtubular memory traces as "a candidate mechanism" and then says plainly that "This mechanism is speculative" and that the dual-domain argument "holds regardless of where the interface is located". `topics/reconsolidation-as-selection-window` (in P-MA1's "applied in" list) reads the lability window as the most concrete place where directional input could enter, "while holding that the directional reading is posited, not proven". Neither entry's credence depends on Tenet 2, so a setback for the quantum interface leaves both untouched. One calibration lag remains (M2). L136 reports Kerskens & López Pérez (2022) without the dispute that at least eight other Map pages record (Warren 2023; single-laboratory, unreplicated), and it cites the single-author Wiest (2025) as "Wiest et al.".

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The anoetic page states the claim in Nagel's own terms: "The jump from noetic to autonoetic is not a change in what the system *does* but in what the system *is like*" (L86). P-MA1 records what is at stake when it calls the pastness quale "a datum rather than an illusion awaiting debunking" (L50). The thought wing's tip-of-the-tongue case gives the best phenomenological evidence in either wing: the meaning is present without the label, "otherwise you couldn't recognise the word as the meaning you possessed when it arrives" (`cognitive-phenomenology` L109). Nagel would also value the memory wing's access reading. Odour-cued memories from the first decade of life, the most semanticised a person has, arrive "with stronger feelings of being 'brought back in time'" (`semanticisation` L70, Willander & Larsson 2006). On that reading the first-person datum is reached by a better route rather than explained away.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

The memory wing rejects the storage model of memory, and Whitehead would welcome that. Semanticisation is presented as transformation ("trace transformation theory treats consolidation as transformation rather than mere stabilisation", `semanticisation` L30), and the consolidation time course is "not a smooth decay" (L68). PCT's constitution relation is "non-causal" and "hyperintensional" (L42–L44). That is the vocabulary of internal relations, not of a thing acting on another thing. **The constraint applies here, and the wing respects it.** Nothing in either entry uses process-philosophical compatibility to raise a grade. The one place where Whitehead-friendly breadth could slip is the anoetic comparative table (L118), which assigns anoetic consciousness to "Most animals" without the "?" the next row carries. That is the Hardline Empiricist's item below, not praise.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Klein's argument that the autonoetic *manner*, rather than the content, drives future-oriented planning (anoetic L110–L112) is the wing's most direct link to agency. "The manner of experiencing—not the information alone—determines what the agent can do with the memory." P-TU1 is careful about effort. It routes "the felt effort of reasoning" to P-A4, so felt intellectual effort "cannot certify its own causal standing" (L46). Kane would praise that. He would also notice that the anoetic Tenet 3 paragraph (L128) says Klein's argument "supports the Map's commitment to mental causation". Klein's argument is neutral between phenomenal and functional readings of autonoesis, and the page itself says he reached it "by conceptual and phenomenological analysis of existing findings rather than by new experiment" (L110). "Is consistent with" is the honest verb (priority item 1b).

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

Both wings admit they cannot settle the question from inside. The thought wing has its "three faces of irresolvability" and the Würzburg–Titchener precedent: "disputes of this structural shape do not get settled by their introspective contestants" (`cognitive-phenomenology` L196). The memory wing says of De Brigard's construct-validity challenge, "The Map has no decisive answer to that and holds the reading defended here at correspondingly moderate confidence" (`phenomenology-of-memory-and-the-self` L123). P-TU1 turns this into a grading rule: an independent marker of phenomenality could raise the grade, but "nothing first-personal can, which is the Würzburg lesson" (L49). McGinn would call it epistemic humility that changes what the register records.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

This persona has the most to praise in the wing.

- **"Unrefuted is not survived."** P-MA1 books an untested falsifier as a weakness of the position. It does not count it as support (L49). The 09-21 audit searched the whole register and found no entry stating a survived-when-untested standing. Two further points are just as disciplined. The falsifier's instrument is specified so that it cannot fake the result: the binary remember/know format is "categorical by construction, a graded fade and a discrete flip generating identical data" (L52). And the access control is made "a condition of the result counting, not a hedge added after the fact" (L52).
- **Declining a shield that was available.** As shown in [The Convergence](#the-convergence), the thought wing's "kind, not degree" reply would have let P-MA1 dismiss any gradient. P-MA1 did not use it and staked itself on a gradient. That is the restraint this persona looks for: a protective move was available, and the entry declined it.
- **Two grains kept separate.** P-TU1 separates the abductive commitment (the constitutive horn, held lower) from what the evidence delivers (constraint, not establishment) and from its causal standing (P-A4). It grades C because "its *phenomenal* interpretation is contested where its architectural reality is not" (L45).
- **Falsifier hygiene in three of four memory-wing pages.** The parent page bounded its relocation reply (L119–L121). `episodic-memory` left out three candidates "already answered by a standing reply above" (L166). `semanticisation` calls functionalist falsifiers "empty" (L44) and states its own conclusion as "a hardening null at a higher stated ceiling" (L56).

**Flags (calibration, not praise):**

1. **Anoetic challenge list** (L134–L141): two struck candidates are still listed, and the two real tests (decomposition, within-memory grading) and the construct-validity limb are missing. The neural-gradient item targets "the qualitative discontinuity argument", which L86 makes explicitly phenomenal. Neural continuity would weaken the separable-substrates claim at L98, and the item should aim there. Priority item 1.
2. **Anoetic table** (L118): "Most animals" for anoetic consciousness, with no hedge, one row above "Higher mammals?". On the five-tier scale, anoetic consciousness across most animal taxa is a live hypothesis at best. "Many animals?" costs 0 words.
3. **`cognitive-phenomenology` L127**: "so reliably" is unsourced and contradicted by the corpus's own `noetic-feelings-void`. Priority item 4.
4. **P-TU1 unification** (carried from `pessimistic-2026-10-01-inference-to-the-best-explanation-against-dualism`, "Recorded, Not Ranked", L240). P-TU1 still argues from unification ("where rivals handle them piecemeal", L46). The IBE page now says the Map's abductive conclusions, "the phenomenal constitution thesis included, are blunted exactly as far as the physicalist's" (IBE L84). PCT L86 has taken on the bad-lot exposure. P-TU1 has not, and the item was never minted because it was off that review's list. Priority item 2.

## Expansion Opportunities

### High Priority

#### Semantic satiation as an inducible test for P-TU1's zero case (research-topic, for the harvester)
- **Builds on**: P-TU1 L49 (the clean zero case), `semanticisation` L72 (the falsifier designed from published parts), `voids/recognition-void` L67 and `topics/memory-anomalies` L61, which already cite Moulin et al. - **Would address**: P-TU1's discriminability is "indirect" because its zero case has to be *found*. Satiation offers one that can be *induced*. It drives a word's felt meaning toward zero within a single item, while competence can be measured on the same trials. If competence falls together with felt meaning, the result fits PCT, though a common cause is equally possible. If competence survives at the satiated endpoint, that is P-TU1's own zero in miniature. The memory wing's lesson carries over: a reinstatement probe is needed to separate loss of access from loss of the phenomenal property.
