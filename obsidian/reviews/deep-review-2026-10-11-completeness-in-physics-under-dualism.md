## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **What the no-go theorems establish: factual overclaim plus internal contradiction (L54, L66, L78, lead).** Four places overstated the theorems:
   - L66: they "close off the possibility that quantum mechanics is an incomplete sketch of deeper, deterministic physics".
   - L78: they show the gap "cannot be filled by more physics of the familiar kind—more forces, particles, or equations of motion".
   - L54: "No additional structural information is missing".
   - Lead: "nothing structural is missing".

   De Broglie–Bohm is a deterministic hidden-variable completion that survives Bell, Kochen-Specker, Hardy and PBR, because it is nonlocal, contextual and ψ-ontic. It adds both an equation of motion (guidance) and extra structure (particle positions). The article's own L104 concedes that Bohm "fixes a determinate outcome… leaving no open selection to make", and that GRW/CSL relocate selection into a stochastic dynamical law. So the article contradicted itself.

   The canonical correction already sits in `concepts/quantum-completeness` (§no-go-theorems, fixed 2026-09-04) and `concepts/physical-completeness` L66. It had never reached this page. **Fixed by propagation:**
   - Lead: "the no-go theorems leave no local, non-contextual or epistemic completion to add".
   - L54: scoped to "of the local, non-contextual or epistemic kind", with PBR's independence assumption made explicit.
   - L64: the reversal is now "short of Bohm's deterministic route", and the results "expose an ontological silence" rather than "establish ontological incompleteness". This also keeps L64 consistent with the CH counter-position, since CH keeps indeterminism but denies there is a gap.
   - L66: "a deeper *local* or *epistemic* physics", plus one sentence saying determinism survives only nonlocally and contextually in de Broglie–Bohm, which the Map declines on tenet grounds rather than no-go grounds.
   - L78: the surviving physical fillings (Bohmian guidance, GRW-type collapse) are now named as known options, surveyed below, rather than awaited discoveries. This keeps the god-of-the-gaps defence honest.

2. **Possibility/probability slippage (§2 diagnostic) at four loci.** In each case the supporting sentences establish only defeater-removal and non-conflict. A reviewer who accepts the tenets would still flag the upgrades. The sibling `quantum-completeness` RTSP already uses the calibrated form ("room, not requirement").
   - Lead: "one of the strongest arguments for it [dualism]" → "it leaves room for dualism and marks where an interacting mind would act". The `description` was changed to match ("supports" → "leaves room for").
   - RTSP Dualism: "the positive case for dualism from physics itself" → "removes physics' strongest objection to dualism and leaves room for it, without compelling it".
   - L72: "Independent evidence for this boundary" → "A suggestive, though not probative, consideration". The paragraph's own conclusion is "may be compatible with—though it does not uniquely demonstrate", so the lead-in had outrun it.
   - L116: "the gap… specifically supports interactionist dualism rather than a monist reinterpretation" → "the Map reads the gap… as room for interactionist dualism". The bidirectional-interaction tenet does the discriminating, not the gap.

3. **Fifth over-concession locus (L48).** The 09-11 repair scoped four loci to the unconditioned register. It missed L48, which said closure and a principled gap "are empirically equivalent at the level of observable statistics". That flatly contradicts L92, which says the conditional register "stays open… a live question that tests the interface itself". Rescoped to "in unconditioned aggregate statistics, and no conditioned test has yet separated them".
