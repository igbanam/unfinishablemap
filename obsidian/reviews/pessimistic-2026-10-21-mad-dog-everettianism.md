### The Eliminative Materialist
Churchland would accept Reading A and go further. On HSF the "I" is a pattern in a vector, and asking which branch *I* am is like asking which of two equally good coarse-grainings is the real whirlpool. She would object that the page treats the Map's "real further fact" as a live option the physics merely fails to supply. In her view the page should say the physics gives no reason to think there is such a fact. The page's RSP (L81) honestly locates the tenet's standing in the non-deflationary subject, so the reply is the tenets page's, and this is bedrock, not a defect.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist
Dennett would note that the page's best weapon for Reading A is his own criterion, quoted through Wallace 2003 at L66. That criterion is twenty years older than HSF and does not need it, so the deflationary reply was already complete without HSF. What HSF adds, that not even space is fundamental, has nothing to do with the self. He would press the lead (L36), which credits HSF with the strongest deflationary reply "since on HSF neither branches nor observers were ever fundamental". That is true of Wallace too (Item 3).

He would also press the Tenet 1 paragraph (L83). If HSF makes the explanatory gap "as sharp as it can be", then the gap's sharpness varies with the physical base. That concedes it is partly a matter of description, which is exactly his diagnosis (Item 4, low).

### The Quantum Skeptic
Tegmark named the factorisation problem as "intimately related to the nature of an observer". He would read limit 4's "like Wallace, it can take the split as an input" (L73) and ask *whose* split. A physicalist observer inherits a split from the physics it is part of. A non-physical subject that selects among einselected outcomes must also say which subsystem is its own, and no derivation of the system/environment split answers that. The Map's own `quantum-factorisation-problem` L76 says exactly this ("a paired subject, unlike a physicalist observer, cannot inherit the split from a physics it is not identical with"). Limit 4 omits it and calls the shared input the debt (Item 1).

### The Many-Worlds Defender
