## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **Attribution and calibration error in §The Penny-Toss Objection (old L67).** The article said the Map's proposal "differs from Compton's amplifier in positing a selector" and that "Popper did not consider this proposal, and nothing in his texts endorses it." The raw text of "Of Clouds and Clocks" says otherwise. Popper's description of the quantum theorists' master-switch model (Objective Knowledge p. 233) already has minds working on bodies "by influencing or selecting some quantum jumps"; he then addresses the refined version, a mind acting "just within the Heisenberg uncertainties", as a "tiny baby theory", conceding "perhaps a slight advance here, in so far as the size of the baby is specified. But I still do not love the baby" (p. 233). His two surviving complaints are Schlick's objection ("it is not clear how they escape from Schlick's objection", p. 232) and grain (the amplifier model "strongly suggests that all our decisions are either snap-decisions ... or else composed of snap-decisions", p. 233). So a selector was inside the family Popper rejected, and the article's claim to have escaped the objection by novelty of mechanism was a possibility/probability slippage a tenet-accepting reviewer would still flag. **Resolution**: paragraph rewritten to quote the three passages, state the two surviving complaints, and make the Map's "intermediate category" candidacy something that has to be earned against both rather than something Popper never considered. Lead sentence and the Minimal Quantum Interaction line in Relation to Site Perspective updated to carry the corrected calibration; the follow-on paragraph now names Schlick's complaint and the grain complaint explicitly instead of restating the grain point as a new "constraint". References entry for Popper 1972 extended to pp. 232–233.
