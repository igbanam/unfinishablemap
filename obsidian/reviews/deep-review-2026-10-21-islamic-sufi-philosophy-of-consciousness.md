## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **Avicenna "holistic" mental causation was transposed from a modern Bohmian model (L47, reused at L103).** The article said Avicenna's "crucial insight, developed further in his *al-Shifā'*", was that mental causation "operates holistically rather than at discrete spatial points". The only cited interpreters are Jamali, Golshani and Jamali, and they reached this point by inference.
   - Their Heliyon companion paper (PMC6667669, raw text grepped) says: "In Avicenna's perspective, mind has an important property which is its non-temporality and space-time independency. **Therefore**, the effects of mind on matter cannot be at a special space-time point".
   - Their model's feature 3 says the effect "does not refer to a specific point in space-time", and they attribute it to "the modified Bohmian quantum potential".
   - Avicenna's own model, on their reading, works "through an intermediate substance". The preprint abstract (Europe PMC PPR81702) describes "a third identity, which works as an interface".
   - **Fix (L47)**: the paragraph now states the intermediary reading (2019a). It quotes the interpreters' inference and the Bohmian implementation (2019b), and labels the holism as their inference and a feature of their model, not a doctrine quoted from Avicenna's text.
   - **Fix (L103)**: Tenet 2 is rewritten as "the nearest contact is modern rather than Avicennan". It now links the [[bohm-implicate-order-and-active-information|determinism-versus-selection tension]], and "the parallel is suggestive at most".
   - The substring `consciousness biasing quantum probability distributions.` was deliberately kept verbatim. The queued P3 Family-3 sweep (`concepts/emergence` task, secondary file L103) holds that exact OLD string.
2. **The al-Bisṭāmī quotation is not verifiable (L83).** The article quoted "I sloughed off my self as a snake sloughs off its skin, and then I looked and saw that I and He were one."
   - Searched in raw full texts: Nicholson, *A Literary History of the Arabs* (1907); Nicholson, *The Mystics of Islam* (1914); Arberry, *Sufism* (1950); and Arberry's *Muslim Saints and Mystics* (Attar). Its "I and He were one" ending appears in none of them.
   - The only web hits for the "sloughed off my self" wording are non-scholarly, and they end it "I saw that I was He".
   - **Fix**: replaced with Nicholson 1907 p. 391, verbatim: "I came forth from Bayazid-ness as a snake from its skin. Then I looked. I saw that lover, beloved, and love are one". Also added Nicholson's caution that the genuineness of the sayings ascribed to him "is not above suspicion", which is verbatim on the same page.
3. **The Qur'an 89:27–28 rendering was unattributed and matches no translation (L63).** "O tranquil self, return to your Lord, well-pleased and well-pleasing." was checked against all 17 English editions on alquran.cloud (Tanzil). None has it verbatim; the closest are Ahmed Ali and Mubarakpuri. The 06-25 ledger's "Quran.com" form also differs. **Fix**: Arberry's verbatim rendering, credited (Arberry 1955).
4. **The Mulla Ṣadrā formula was uncredited and its transliteration was mis-formed (L51).** The English "corporeal in its origination and spiritual in its subsistence" is verbatim in Kalin 2010, pp. 274–275 (Google Books search-within on id `2oM8DwAAQBAJ`, control "substantial motion" 9 hits). The SEP's version, which the article implied, differs ("…spiritual [or incorporeal] in its survival"). The transliteration *jismānī al-ḥudūth, rūḥānī al-baqā'* matches neither source: Kalin pp. 10 and 138 have *jismaniyyat al-huduth ruhaniyyat al-baqa'*, and the SEP has *…wa-ruhaniyyat…*. **Fix**: the rendering is now credited to Kalin with pages, and the transliteration is corrected to *jismāniyyat al-ḥudūth rūḥāniyyat al-baqā'*. Family check: `topics/the-steelman-for-process-monism` L59 uses Kalin's p. 162 variant ("material in its origination…"). Both are verbatim Kalin, so no new variant was minted.
5. **Attribution: *waḥdat al-wujūd* (L91).** The article called it "his doctrine". Chittick's SEP entry (rev. 5 Dec 2025), §3.1, says calling him its founder "is misleading, for he never uses the expression". **Fix**: "the teaching later labelled *waḥdat al-wujūd* … an expression he never used himself (Chittick 2008/2025)".
6. **Calibration: Tenet 3 overclaim (L105).** "Provides the tradition's strongest case for bidirectional causation" and "adds experiential evidence" put an evidence claim inside a section that opens by disclaiming support. **Fix**: changed to "the tradition's clearest statement" and "adds a physiological record … That record fits the tenet without evidencing it".

### Medium Issues Found
- **The Flying Man was called a "demonstration" (L45) and a "proof" (L37).** Adamson (Aeon, 9 Sep 2016; verified at aeon.co, `datePublished` 2016-09-09) writes: "Avicenna calls it a *tanbīh*, meaning a 'pointer' to something". He calls the thought experiment a challenge "to his materialist opponents". **Fix**: changed to "is offered to show…", with the *tanbīh*/"pointer" line cited inline (Adamson 2016). L37 "proof" changed to "case". This also gives Adamson 2016 its first inline cite.
- **The *Munqidh* does not mention *murāqaba* (L49).** Watt's translation names the "recollection" of God (*dhikr*) repeatedly, and has no *murāqaba*. **Fix**: changed to "above all the Sufi *dhikr*, the heart's recollection of God".
- **Unsupported generalisations.** L65 "These are not theoretical postulates but descriptions grounded in contemplative observation across generations of practitioners" (an unsupported claim in not-X-but-Y form) was changed to "Sufi teachers present them as stations reached in practice rather than as theoretical postulates". L85 "practitioners consistently report" was changed to "Sufi accounts describe".
- **Anachronism (L45).** "The soul determines brain states" was changed to "governs the body through the practical intellect".
- **An unsourced appeal to scholars (L77).** "has been noted by scholars of comparative contemplative traditions" was trimmed.
