## Critiques by Philosopher

### The Eliminative Materialist (Patricia Churchland)
This edit is what a research programme does when it is protecting a posit rather than testing it. Before 17:25 the corridor was at least exposed at the public grain — condition on task, on subject, on a pre-cued goal, look for a residual. Now every grain a neuroscientist could actually record is Born-exact "by construction", and the only distribution the selector is allowed to disturb is one conditioned on a variable the Map's own agency-budget page says must be "publicly unresolvable in principle" (L87). The vocabulary of selection has been moved to where no measurement can reach it, and the register calls that no change in confidence.

### The Hard-Nosed Physicalist (Daniel Dennett)
"Only conditionals on the conscious state itself are free." Fine — write one down. A conditional P(O | C, X) where C is, by the register's new stipulation, not a context anyone can condition on is a distribution with no sample space anyone can construct. The "freedom" being preserved is the freedom of an empty set. Worse for the Map: heterophenomenology works entirely with reports, and a report is a public label. If reported intention is a publicly conditionable context, the new P-Q2 says outcome frequencies conditioned on it are Born-exact, so the selector's content cannot show up in what subjects say they were trying to do. That is the self-stultification result the selection-only-channel page spends a section trying to avoid (L94), now entailed by the register.

### The Quantum Skeptic (Max Tegmark)
The strong quantifier is justified by case 2 of the September deep review: a mind holding C=0 through one block and C=1 through another steers the distant wing of an entangled pair. Where, in a warm brain at 310 K, is the entangled distant wing? The decoherence story the Map uses to prefer post-decoherence selection (P-Q1) is precisely that brain-internal branch structure is not entangled with anything outside the skull by the time selection acts. So the register has tightened its central position to defend against a Bell-pair-in-the-brain scenario its own decoherence account says will not arise, and paid for it with every public-grain prediction it had. If the signalling worry is real for some system, say which; if it is not, the quantifier is a free gift to horn (b).

### The Many-Worlds Defender (David Deutsch)
The whole agony over *which* ensemble Born-exactness is asserted in is an artefact of refusing branching. On the unitary picture there is no marginal to preserve and nothing to select; the Born weights are the branch measure and the question "pooled or per-context" never arises. What the register has now written — Born statistics in every public context, a hidden variable C that is free but unobservable — is a hidden-variables theory with a privacy postulate, and P-Q11 already concedes that in that class exactness is forced by signal-locality. The Map has arrived at Valentini's structure without Valentini's dynamics, and declines the one interpretation on which the problem dissolves.

### The Empiricist (Karl Popper's Ghost)
Here the edit changes the Map's falsifiability profile and the register does not say so. Tenet 2's own falsifier (c) tests "the endorsed corridor path only through its conditional structure" (L81), and L75 and L107 name that structure: a deviation "conditioned on intention, task or subject". After 17:25 a deviation conditioned on task or subject is "the minimum-outside-the-corridor fallback … not a corridor prediction" (calibration note). The second prediction of `apex/self-concealing-interface` — I(outcome ; preregistered intention | physical state) > 0 — is conditioned on a *preregistered, pre-cued* goal, a public label; on the new reading its positive result does not confirm the corridor, it refutes it. P-Q9 can no longer say "this is not a global unfalsifiability shield" of the default reading, because the three channels it names are now (i) a Route-2 test, (ii) tests of rival mechanisms, and (iii) a psychophysical claim about qualia inversion that does not touch Born statistics at all. The honest statement is: the default reading makes no public-grain statistical prediction; its empirical exposure lives entirely in a fallback the Map does not endorse.

### The Buddhist Philosopher (Nagarjuna)
"The conscious state itself" is doing a great deal of work for something the register treats as unanalysed. It is the one variable the world is permitted to be conditioned on and no one is permitted to observe — a self with causal privileges and no public identity. The privacy commitment the agency-budget page says "is a question for the positions register" (L87) is the atman written as a quantifier restriction: a permanent, inward, inaccessible subject whose existence is secured by stipulating that nothing public can index it. A Madhyamaka reader would ask what remains of *selection by the conscious state* when the conscious state is defined by its inaccessibility to every context — and would note that the subject-census register (P-SC1) already requires C to be indexed to a subject, a moment and a phenomenal state, which is to say, to things with public correlates.
