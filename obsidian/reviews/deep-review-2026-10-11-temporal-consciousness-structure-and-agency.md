4. **Csikszentmihalyi (1990): the reported result was wrong (leg 7).** L142 read: "Csikszentmihalyi's experience-sampling research found this repeatedly: people in flow cannot estimate how much time has passed." The raw text (*Flow*, archive.org djvu full text, §"The Transformation of Time", grep-verified) says: "One of the most common descriptions of optimal experience is that time no longer seems to pass the way it ordinarily does … Often hours seem to pass by in minutes; in general, most people report that time seems to pass much faster. The book reports a misjudged duration, not an inability to estimate. The time dimension comes from interview material, not specifically from experience sampling. **Fixed**: the sentence now reports the source accurately, keeps his caveat, and scales the Map's reading down from "metric comparison becomes impossible" to "a restructuring of the present, not merely a misjudged interval". The 07-13 ledger line "real-correct … loss of time-tracking is a canonical flow dimension" had approved the paraphrase without checking the raw text.
