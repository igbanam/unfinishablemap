## Reply

# Hostile-Referee Audit of The Unfinishable Map (live site, checked 15–20 October 2026)

The Map’s most original contribution is how it audits itself: the positions register, the “bias-without-deviation” dilemma, and the open admission that no toy model of its mechanism exists. That auditing has not reached the corpus as a whole. Older and secondary articles still overstate the quantum case, contradict the tenets page on the Zeno mechanism, decoherence numbers and quantum biology, and misdescribe at least three primary sources. The quantum-interface position, on the Map’s own Born-rule reading, also concedes the probabilistic form of causal closure that Kim and Papineau actually need.

## TL;DR

- **Novelty.** The real advances are methodological and diagnostic: the calibrated positions register (P-Q2, P-Q3, P-Q10), the “self-concealing interface” and conditioned-Born-deviation framing, the possibility/probability-slippage discipline, and parts of the voids catalogue. The argumentative core (zombies, Mary, Elitzur’s self-stultification, Stapp/Wigner quantum interactionism, Chisholm/Lowe/O’Connor agent causation, Schurger on Libet) restates Chalmers, Jackson, Popper–Eccles, Stapp and Swinburne without adding to them.
- **Coherence.** The tenets page is now careful. It concedes indistinguishability from chance “by construction” and ranks Stapp’s Zeno model behind post-decoherence selection. Many downstream pages have not caught up: they call Stapp “the most explicit implementation” of Tenet 2, cite photosynthetic coherence as live evidence even though another Map page notes it was overturned (Cao et al. 2020, *Science Advances*, report “the absence of long-lived interexciton coherence on relevant time scales”), give the Hagan et al. correction as seven versus eight-to-nine orders of magnitude, and argue from parsimony while Tenet 5 disarms parsimony.
- **Citations and steelmen.** I found real errors: Schurger’s “Libetus interruptus” task is misdescribed, Maoz et al.’s arbitrary condition is miscast as “donation choices”, Proietti et al. 2019 is presented as empirically “confirming” Frauchiger–Renner contradictions, and Frankish is said to predict that the illusion should “dissolve” (he says the opposite). The replies to illusionism and causal closure are weaker than the Map believes.

## Key Findings

### 1. Novelty: what adds something and what recapitulates

**Genuinely novel or distinctive (keep and foreground):**

- **The positions register and mechanism-debt convention.** Tenets now concede that P-Q3 “sits genuinely close to epiphenomenalism” and that P-Q10 records “no worked toy model of the coupling exists” ([tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)). [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) I know of no comparable public dualist project that grades its own central mechanism “framework-internal only”. This is the site’s best feature.
- **The scoped indistinguishability claim.** Selection is “empirically indistinguishable from chance… by construction”, but “a deviation *conditioned* on intention, task or subject would test the corridor itself” ([tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)). [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) Separating unconditioned from conditioned Born deviations is a sharp, testable reframing of the old “psi-free interactionism” problem. It is more precise than Stapp’s or Eccles’s treatments.
- **Tenet 5’s symmetric self-binding.** The Map declines to use parsimony against Many-Worlds because it disarms parsimony against dualism (“the Map cannot invoke simplicity against many-worlds while disarming parsimony arguments *against* dualism”, [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)). [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) This is principled and uncommon.
- **Possibility/probability slippage.** “A tenet may remove a defeater, but it must not upgrade the evidence level” ([concepts/possibility-probability-slippage](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/possibility-probability-slippage/)) is a useful methodological contribution. [concepts/possibility-probability-slippage](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/possibility-probability-slippage/)
- **Some voids.** The 10-15 impossible-percept research note’s observation that impossible percepts invert the Map’s own “refer-but-not-grasp” asymmetry (“grasp-but-not-refer”) is a real conceptual move ([workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)). [workflow/changelog](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

[concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns/) Its novelty lies in the careful hedging, not the idea.

### 6. Steelmen the Map answers too quickly

**Papineau/Kim, probabilistic closure.** Papineau’s mature closure principle is probabilistic. In “The Causal Closure of the Physical and Naturalism” (*Oxford Handbook of Philosophy of Mind*, 2009) he writes that “quantum mechanics still specifies that random physical effects have their probabilities fixed by sufficient immediate physical causes”, and that any sui generis cause “would have to make a difference to the probability of the relevant physical effect”. The Map’s endorsed reading (P-Q2) preserves Born statistics exactly, and the Free Will topic concedes that “Born-preserving selection leaves probabilistic closure intact”. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)[topics/free-will](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/free-will/) So the closure premise the physicalist needs is granted. The remaining “selection” is a token-level fact with no difference to any physical chance. That is precisely Kim’s exclusion picture, with the mental as an idle actualiser. Calling it “trumping preemption” does not help: trumping requires that the trumping cause make a counterfactual difference somewhere, and the Map has ruled that out for every unconditioned test. P-Q3’s admission that the selection “sits genuinely close to epiphenomenalism” ([tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)) is the honest statement. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) The articles that say quantum mechanics “is not causally closed in the way materialism requires” ([arguments/materialism-argument](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/materialism-argument/)) should be rewritten to match it. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/)

**Tegmark, properly read.** Tegmark’s conclusion is that cognitively relevant degrees of freedom are classical: “there is nothing fundamentally wrong with the current classical approach to neural network simulations” ([https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9907009](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9907009)). [arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9907009) Post-decoherence selection does not “sidestep” this, as testing-consciousness-collapse claims. [topics/testing-consciousness-collapse](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/testing-consciousness-collapse/) It concedes it, then adds an unobservable actualisation step on top of a classical dynamics that already fully explains behaviour. The strongest form of the objection is that the Map’s endorsed mechanism is explanatorily idle for neuroscience. The Map’s best reply is P-Q10’s “no toy model”, which is an admission, not a rebuttal.

**Frankish and Dennett.** The Map’s two anti-illusionist arguments are:

- “the representing state and the represented state coincide”, so no independent vantage exists;
- the illusion should dissolve under attention. [arguments/materialism-argument](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/materialism-argument/)

The second misreads Frankish (see §5). The first assumes what is at issue: illusionists hold that introspective self-models are ordinary representations that can be wrong about their own vehicles, as any self-monitoring system can. The strongest version is a predictive-processing self-model. Its interoceptive and attentional priors predict both the persistence of the seeming and its resistance to theoretical knowledge. Two examples are Graziano’s attention schema and the 2021 *Neuroscience of Consciousness* model by Sandved-Smith, Hesp, Mattout, Friston, Lutz & Ramstead, which casts “mental action as policy selection over higher-level cognitive states”. The Map acknowledges active inference models “the bandwidth bottleneck, the felt cost of holding attention, and the coarse-grained allocation of detail… without invoking anything non-physical” ([concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns/)) but still lists “the phenomenology of effort persists” as a reply to illusionism. [concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns/) Effort phenomenology is exactly what that model predicts.

**Self-stultification against illusionism.** The Libet page claims “The illusionist cannot coherently claim to have *reasoned* to their position” ([concepts/libet-experiments](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/libet-experiments/)). [concepts/libet-experiments](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/libet-experiments/) Illusionism denies phenomenal properties, not reasoning. Inference is a functional and representational process the illusionist fully retains. The tenets page itself concedes that the phenomenal-concept strategy survives self-stultification and that illusionism must be met “at a framework boundary”. [tenets](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)[arguments/materialism-argument](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/materialism-argument/) The Libet page should not claim a refutation the tenets page disowns.

**Churchland and the “dissolution test”.** Against Materialism argues that contemplative training “should dissolve the seeming the way scientific instruction dissolved phlogiston-talk”. [arguments/materialism-argument](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/materialism-argument/) Churchland’s reply is that perceptual and introspective categories are retrained by theory-laden practice, not by verbal instruction. Long-term meditators’ reports do shift: the Map’s own text concedes “the tradition’s own analysis of *vedanā* points toward *anattā*”. [arguments/materialism-argument](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/materialism-argument/) The evidence the Map cites is therefore at best neutral.
