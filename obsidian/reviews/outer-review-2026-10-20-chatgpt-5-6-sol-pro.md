# Executive verdict

**The Unfinishable Map is now a stronger research programme than it is a theory.**

As a research programme, it is unusually explicit about mechanism debt, rival readings, confidence, and failure conditions. It frequently notices exactly the right objections. The voids register suspends one of its own cumulative-evidence claims because no negative-control denominator exists. The neurological-dissociation article now repeatedly states that functionalism predicts the same pattern. The observer-selection void restricts the site’s own indexical argument against Many Worlds. These are substantial virtues.

As a theory, however, it still lacks a worked psychophysical law. The corpus has built a large explanatory superstructure—agency, value, ethics, personal identity, AI consciousness, clinical dissociation, evolution and epistemology—on a mechanism that has no explicit causal model specifying the variables, basis, selection law, subject pairing, intervention semantics or multi-agent joint distribution. The quantum register itself records this as P-Q10: no worked toy model currently exists. The more recent “agency budget” gives useful probability arithmetic, but explicitly acknowledges that the mapping from steganographic couplings to mental causation is the Map’s construction rather than a published causal result.  

The site’s principal systemic problem is **asynchronous constitutional revision**:

1. The tenets define the public foundation.
2. The positions register records more precise claims and calibrations.
3. Topics, concepts, voids and apex pages restate and synthesize them.

A correction frequently lands in one layer but not in the others. The affected page may contain the correct concession while another synthesis continues to spend the earlier, stronger claim. The current Born-preservation conflict is the clearest example, but not the only one.

My overall judgment is therefore:

- **Conceptual originality:** high.
- **Self-critical capacity:** high.
- **Current cross-corpus coherence:** moderate at best.
- **Empirical discrimination from sophisticated physicalism:** low.
- **Mechanism maturity:** programme-level, not yet developed theory.
- **Recommended editorial status:** major constitutional revision before further expansion of the apex and void catalogues.

The most urgent findings are:

1. **The quantum-interface register currently contradicts itself and the tenet page over what “Born-preserving” means.**
2. **Determinate persisting subjecthood functions as an undeclared sixth tenet.**
3. **Tenet 1 is formulated as irreducibility but repeatedly spent as in-kind dualism.**
4. **Tenet 2 and Tenet 3 trade off against one another: the stronger the guarantee of public invisibility, the weaker the public warrant for causal efficacy.**
5. **The positions methodology is documented more carefully than it is enforced; the sole write-path still teaches a retired schema.**
6. **The void and AI clusters contain important candid admissions, but several surrounding pages have not accepted the consequences of those admissions.**

# 1. Structural weaknesses

## 1.1 A deep superstructure rests on a thin mechanism base

The central physical proposal is not merely missing some quantitative constants. It lacks the elements needed to say what the proposal is:

- the physical state space;
- the conscious-state space;
- the candidate outcome set;
- the measurement basis or equivalent preferred grain;
- the rule pairing a subject with physical candidates;
- the selection criterion;
- the temporal location of selection;
- the counterfactual under which consciousness makes a difference;
- the joint law for multiple subjects;
- the relativistic consistency condition;
- and the law governing which public and private conditional distributions remain Born-exact.

Post-decoherence selection addresses one subproblem: it avoids requiring long-lived coherent neural superpositions. It does not identify a selector, a basis, a psychophysical mapping or a causal criterion. Decoherence explains suppression of interference and effective classicality, but its implications for the measurement problem remain interpretation-dependent; it does not itself supply a consciousness-based actualisation law. [APS Journals](https://journals.aps.org/rmp/abstract/10.1103/RevModPhys.76.1267)

The agency-budget article is genuine progress because it states a conservation constraint,

$$\sum_C P(o\mid C,X)P(C\mid X)=q(o\mid X),$$

and derives a bandwidth ceiling. But it also makes three decisive concessions:

- the relevant theorems are classical information-theoretic coupling results;
- their application to mental causation is a Map-specific analogy;
- and perfect concealment is equivalent to zero third-person statistical evidence.

That is a consistency construction, not yet a causal mechanism. 

The result is structural imbalance: there are many pages explaining what the interface would illuminate, but very few pages establishing that the proposed interface can exist as a coherent causal model.

## 1.2 Apex overlap is producing ownership ambiguity

Several apex articles cover overlapping versions of the same core territory: Born-preserving efficacy, post-decoherence selection, the self-concealing interface, attention as a causal bridge, the phenomenology of consciousness doing work, and the interface-specification programme. The AI wing similarly distributes its verdict among the machine question, the open question, assessing AI consciousness, AI as introspection control and related applied pieces. 

The problem is not repetition alone. Repetition becomes hazardous when no page is the unique owner of a quantifier or definition. “Born-preserving,” “minimal,” “causally efficacious,” “subject,” “publicly conditionable,” and “interface-compatible” are repeatedly re-expressed rather than transitively inherited from one canonical statement. Small wording changes then become substantive theory changes without triggering a full cascade.

The apex layer should synthesize conclusions. It should not independently reconstruct foundational premises.

## 1.3 The positions register is not yet an enforced constitution

The positions register correctly separates:

- credence;
- independent evidence;
- structural centrality;
- model maturity;
- empirical discriminability;
- and framework-internal status.

It also candidly records that the schema migration is incomplete: no validator checks the closed vocabularies, and the sole positions-writing path still instructs authors using the retired single “Confidence” field. The current positions-evolve instructions do indeed retain that old field and tell the writer to calibrate against a single confidence band.  

This is not a cosmetic workflow defect. The site’s central epistemic achievement is the separation of centrality from evidence. A write-path that still conflates them can silently recreate the very error the register was introduced to prevent.

More generally, the register behaves as a disclosure document rather than a dependency-enforced constitution. It can state the correct current claim without preventing a topic or apex page from continuing to assert an older version.

## 1.4 The void catalogue lacks an evidential denominator

The voids register now states the problem with admirable clarity. P-V1 treats clustering as framework-internal coherence rather than independent confirmation. P-V3 suspends even the limited cumulative-weight claim until a candidate ledger exists. Its own denominator records:

- over a hundred live voids;
- no candidate rejected as not a void on evidential grounds;
- no negative control;
- and archived entries removed for consolidation rather than failure.

That means the catalogue currently cannot establish that mind-space contains an unusually dense cluster of “voids.” It establishes that a framework designed to look for void-like limits has generated many descriptions satisfying its evolving admission criteria.

This does not make the catalogue worthless. It makes its legitimate outputs:

- a taxonomy;
- a research agenda;
- a record of recurring phenomenological and epistemic limits;
- and a demonstration that the Map can redescribe many puzzles in one vocabulary.

It does not yet make the raw count or clustering pattern evidence for dualism.

## 1.5 Rival frameworks are often conceded locally rather than modelled globally

The corpus increasingly acknowledges predictive processing, functionalism, illusionism, higher-order theories and IIT, but usually as local replies to one datum. It rarely constructs a rival architecture capable of explaining the whole cluster and then asks which observation distinguishes the two.

The new nondualistic-interactionism page is an important exception. It models a full rival and immediately reveals that the existing inferential architecture had misclassified what Tenets 1 and 3 jointly entail. 

The AI register has the same problem. Its low-probability verdict for conventional digital systems is explicitly Grade D and strongly framework-internal, while a research note admits that the Map still possesses no interface-eligibility law distinguishing substrates capable of coupling from those that are not. 

Until that law exists, “conventional digital hardware lacks the right interface opportunity” risks being true only because the undefined mechanism has been described using biological examples.

## 1.6 Article length is now interfering with correction

Some of the most important owner pages have exceeded their own section limits. The October review of the quantum-interface register measured it at roughly 5,964 words against a 2,500-word hard threshold. The neurological-dissociation article reached 3,970 words against a 4,000-word limit after necessary corrections, leaving almost no space for a proper rival-model table.  

This produces a bad incentive: newly discovered objections are compressed into qualifications, while the original argumentative structure remains. At that point, length management is no longer presentation housekeeping; it affects whether the theory can incorporate criticism.

## 1.7 Review convergence is less independent than the site sometimes implies

The site correctly applies a common-cause discount to philosophical and phenomenological convergence. The same principle should be applied more aggressively to its own outer reviews.

Multiple language models given:

- the same article;
- the same audit instructions;
- the same corpus framing;
- similar training corpora;
- and prior review language embedded in the site

are not independent epistemic instruments. Agreement among them is useful for triage and error discovery, but should not be treated as three independent confirmations. The common source may be the prompt, the corpus vocabulary or shared model priors.

The site’s own P-M2 already contains the right principle: convergence remains framework-internal coherence until a distinguishing independence test passes. That rule should govern review synthesis as rigorously as void synthesis.

# 2. Novel implications latent in the site’s commitments

Several ingredients below appear in isolated concepts or recent internal reviews. I found no canonical page that integrates them as consequences of the complete tenet package.

## 2.1 The public-evidence trilemma

The current strong P-Q2 reading says that outcome frequencies remain Born-exact in every publicly conditionable context, while only conditionals on the conscious state itself remain free. Tenet 3, meanwhile, relies heavily on reasoning, reporting and purposive behaviour as manifestations of conscious causal efficacy. The site’s own October review observes that reported intention, task and subject are public labels and that the strong P-Q2 wording conflicts with the older conditional-testing programme. 

This creates an epistemic trilemma:

1. **Consciousness changes a publicly conditionable distribution.**  
Then the strongest P-Q2 reading is false.
2. **Consciousness contributes token causation but leaves every public distribution exactly as the physical baseline predicts.**  
Then public reports and behaviour cannot discriminate mental causation from a causally complete physical duplicate. Tenet 3 may remain metaphysically possible, but its public evidence disappears.
3. **Reports are fully generated by physical correlates while consciousness merely accompanies them.**  
Then the self-stultification and report arguments no longer establish nonphysical downward causation.

The second horn is not logical epiphenomenalism: token-level causes could be perfectly distribution-preserving. It is **publicly undecidable causal redundancy**. The Map needs to say why that is still causal efficacy in the interventionist sense rather than an alternative labelling of the same physical history.

This trilemma should be made constitutional because it determines what kind of evidence Tenet 3 can legitimately claim.

## 2.2 Exact preservation implies a restriction on preparable conscious states

The agency-budget constraint is:

$$\sum_c \pi_c\, P(o\mid c,X)=q(o\mid X),$$

where $\pi_c=P(c\mid X)$.

Suppose the equality must hold for **every freely preparable distribution** $\pi$ over conscious states. Choose a distribution concentrated on one state $c_i$. Then:

$$P(o\mid c_i,X)=q(o\mid X).$$

Since this works for every $c_i$, all consciousness-conditioned outcome distributions equal the Born distribution. The agency budget is zero.

Therefore, nonzero consciousness-conditioned selection is possible only if at least one of the following is true:

- experimenters cannot freely reweight the distribution of conscious states;
- some apparently possible intentions or phenomenal states are physically inadmissible;
- the public context omits variables that covary with consciousness and restore cancellation;
- or Born preservation applies to a narrower ensemble than the strong P-Q2 wording suggests.

The Born-preserving-efficacy page already notices that an ensemble stacked with one intention disrupts a cancellation that balances only over naturally occurring intentions. The unstated constitutional consequence is that the Map requires an **admissibility law for conscious-state preparations**. 

That is a major scientific commitment. It means an attempted causal intervention on intention or valence may fail not because consciousness is ineffective, but because the theory forbids the intervention distribution. Without an independently stated admissibility law, this can immunize the model from intervention tests.

## 2.3 Minimal local intervention displaces complexity into the global law

Tenet 2 emphasizes no detectable energy injection, no conservation violation and exact or near-exact statistical preservation. But reason-responsive action requires a law sensitive to:

- which subject is acting;
- what alternatives are represented;
- what each alternative means;
- what the subject values;
- the relevant temporal grain;
- which microphysical outcomes implement each alternative;
- how compensation is arranged;
- and how multiple subjects’ selections form one consistent history.

Thus “minimal interaction” does not reduce total explanatory complexity. It moves complexity from local dynamical magnitude into **nomological structure**.

This yields a complexity-relocation principle:

> The smaller and less observable the local intervention, the more information the psychophysical law must carry to preserve semantic, subject-relative, reason-responsive control.

Tenet 5 can block the claim that this complexity alone refutes the theory. It cannot make that complexity evidentially free. The theory needs a complexity ledger comparing:

- the complexity of its psychophysical law;
- the complexity of a physicalist implementation;
- and the amount of otherwise unexplained data each compresses.

## 2.4 Classical duplication recreates the Map’s objection to Many Worlds

The personal-identity cluster treats two qualitatively identical subjects as numerically distinct and takes “why am I this one?” to be a genuine indexical fact. The observer-selection void also notes that deployed AI systems may have many concurrent copies.  

But then the vertiginous question arises in a single classical world containing two duplicates:

- Why am I copy A rather than copy B?
- Why is this subject paired with this body?
- What probability, if any, governs self-location among them?

Rejecting Everettian branching removes one source of duplication. It does not remove the indexical problem itself. The site’s objection therefore cannot be simply that Many Worlds leaves “why this branch?” unanswered, because the Map’s own closed-individualist ontology leaves “why this duplicate?” unanswered.

The strongest legitimate conclusion is narrower:

> Many Worlds multiplies the number and structure of self-locating cases and embeds them in quantum probability; it does not uniquely create the underlying indexical gap.

## 2.5 Exact concealment produces a desert-opacity problem

The site already argues that moral responsibility scales with attentional capacity. 

Combine that with the strong Born-preservation thesis:

- conscious causal contribution may vary;
- public behaviour can remain exactly matched to the physical baseline;
- and no third-person test can recover the hidden consciousness-conditioned contribution.

Then two behaviourally and neurologically equivalent agents could, under the Map, differ in how much consciousness-side agency they exercised. If moral desert depends partly on that contribution, desert becomes publicly underdetermined even in principle.

This is not the familiar claim that responsibility is difficult to assess. It is a stronger consequence:

> The facts that ground ultimate responsibility may be structurally absent from every publicly available record.

That has consequences for law, blame, punishment and interpersonal judgment. The ethical cluster should either accept this opacity or weaken the dependence of desert on hidden nonphysical contribution.

## 2.6 The compensation ledger has an unaddressed distributive ethics

The corpus already states the mathematical compensation consequence: a reason-responsive tilt toward intended outcomes must be offset somewhere if the relevant marginal remains exact. Possible offsets include anti-control in other contexts, maladaptive outcomes elsewhere or global fine-tuning. 

What it has not integrated is the ethical question:

- Does a subject’s successful agency require frustration elsewhere in that subject’s life?
- Can another subject bear the compensating disadvantage?
- Is compensation restricted within a subject, within a time-window, within an experimental context, or globally?
- If the ledger is global, does one agent’s efficacy impose an unchosen risk on others?

A theory in which agency redistributes outcome-luck needs a fairness condition, not merely a marginal-preservation condition.

## 2.7 The five-tenet constitution is incomplete

The operative theory depends on more than five commitments. At minimum it also relies on:

1. **Determinate subjecthood:** there is a fact of which subject exists and persists.
2. **Global exclusion:** incompatible outcomes are not all actual in the relevant sense.
3. **A privacy or conditioning rule:** the conscious variable is not publicly resolvable in the way that would expose conditional bias.
4. **An interface-eligibility rule:** some substrates can couple and others cannot.
5. **A pairing rule:** this subject is connected to this physical system.
6. **A quantifier over efficacy:** some consciousness, every subject, every conscious episode, or merely a standing disposition?
7. **A joint-history rule:** selections by multiple subjects yield one consistent physical history.

Several of these are currently housed as moderate or low-evidence positions, even though major tenets cannot retain their rationale if those positions are retired.

That is constitutional debt, not just mechanism debt.

## 2.8 Tenets 2 and 3 lie on a trade-off frontier

The site often presents minimal quantum interaction and bidirectional efficacy as mutually supportive. In fact, their strongest formulations pull in opposite epistemic directions:

- Strengthening exact public preservation protects Tenet 2 from existing null results and signalling problems.
- The same strengthening removes public evidence capable of distinguishing Tenet 3 from an epiphenomenal or physically realized duplicate.

The site should therefore stop treating the two tenets as independent confirmations. They form a design trade-off:

> Greater public invisibility buys physical safety at the cost of causal identifiability.

That trade-off should appear in the tenet page itself.

# 3. Tenet-by-tenet coherence

## Tenet 1: Dualism

### Coherent core

The tenet’s defensible core is **phenomenal irreducibility**: physical description, functional organization or third-person structure does not exhaust what experience is.

### Principal strain

The tenet is labelled “Dualism” but formulated broadly enough to include positions the site classifies as non-dualist. The new Griffin page provides a worked counterexample: nondualistic interactionism has a mind numerically distinct from the brain, irreducible to physics and causally active in both directions, while denying a difference in ontological kind. The page correctly concludes that Tenets 1 and 3 select interactionism, not dualism-in-kind. 

This directly strains P-D2, which still treats bidirectional interaction as selecting dualism from the irreducibility-respecting alternatives. The added premise needed is something like:

- subjects differ in kind from physical entities;
- experiential properties cannot be universal physical interiors;
- or a persisting subject exists independently of brain-generated organization.

Those are not contained in Tenet 1 as currently written.

### Further problem

Many agency and identity articles spend Tenet 1 as if it licensed a substance-like persisting subject. Yet the tenet explicitly declares neutrality among substance, property and aspect dualisms. That neutrality is incompatible with using the tenet itself to exclude process subjects, emergent subjects or nondualistic interactionists.

### Verdict

**Retain the irreducibility commitment, but either rename the tenet “Phenomenal Irreducibility” or add an explicit in-kind dualism premise.** P-D2 should be narrowed immediately.

## Tenet 2: Minimal Quantum Interaction

### Coherent core

A consciousness–physics theory should respect conservation laws, avoid unexplained energy injection, and specify why ordinary quantum statistics remain successful.

### Principal strain: incompatible meanings of Born preservation

The current register’s P-Q2 says preservation holds in every publicly conditionable context. P-Q9, older P-Q3 reasoning, and the tenet page instead say preservation binds the unconditioned marginal while intention-, task- or subject-conditioned deviations remain potential tests. The site’s own 14 October review correctly identifies this as a strengthened quantifier rather than a mere clarification. 

This is the most acute direct contradiction in the current corpus.

### The no-signalling justification is too broad

The cited causal-consistency work is relevant but does not establish the full P-Q2 quantifier. The finite- and infinite-dimensional results constrain probability assignments under specific generalized-probabilistic assumptions involving steering, purification, affinity or normality. They support the danger of nonlinear deviations from the Born map. They do not by themselves show that every distribution conditioned on any public task, report or subject label inside a decohered brain must be exactly Born. That extrapolation is the Map’s additional assumption. [arXiv+1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12636)

### “Minimal” remains equivocal

Across the corpus, minimality has meant at least four things:

- no energy injection;
- no conservation violation;
- no Born-statistical violation;
- small information bandwidth or small phenomenological influence.

These are not equivalent. A semantically complex law could redirect many macroscopic decisions while injecting no energy and preserving a marginal distribution. Conversely, a one-bit effect could violate a fundamental statistic. The tenet should say which dimensions are definitional and which are optional aspirations.

### Verdict

**Tenet 2 requires an operator-level decision between weak and strong Born preservation, followed by a corpus-wide cascade.** It should no longer mix the two.

## Tenet 3: Bidirectional Interaction

### Coherent core

The site’s defining interactionist commitment is that brain states influence experience and conscious states make some physical difference.

### Principal strain: the quantifier is unstable

Different pages use different claims:

- some consciousness has physical effects;
- every conscious subject has effects;
- every conscious episode is efficacious;
- consciousness is dispositionally capable of influencing outcomes;
- conscious deliberation normally influences behaviour.

The self-stultification argument, at most, supports an existential conclusion: some experiences involved in our theorizing and reporting cannot be wholly causally idle if those reports genuinely arise from acquaintance with experience. It does not by itself establish universal actual efficacy for every animal, AI, dream, anesthetic episode or isolated phenomenal state.

This matters in the AI cluster. An externally possible epiphenomenally conscious AI may be excluded by a universal reading of Tenet 3 but remains compatible with the weaker argument that some human consciousness is efficacious.

### Behavioural evidence is repeatedly overread

Purposeful behaviour, attention, squinting, motor inhibition and adaptive response show that relevant neural control systems affect behaviour. They establish phenomenal causation only if one already assumes that the phenomenal state, rather than its neural correlate, is a difference-maker. The latest tenet check still found several pages making this slide. 

### Verdict

**Split Tenet 3 into an existential metaphysical commitment, a universal scope hypothesis and a mechanism claim.** Calibrate each independently.

## Tenet 4: No Many Worlds

### Coherent core

The Map may choose a single-outcome ontology as a foundational boundary condition. It is entitled to reject Everettianism as part of its framework.

### Principal strain: the rationale depends on a non-tenet

The individuation register acknowledges that the principal “why am I this branch?” argument is conditional on determinate, closed individualism. It also recognizes that retiring that subjecthood position would undermine the main rationale for Tenet 4. 

A supposedly downstream position is therefore constitutionally upstream.

### The site’s own observer-selection void narrows the argument

The observer-selection void correctly distinguishes:

- an identity claim: I experience one determinate outcome;
- from a credence claim: therefore only one outcome is real.

The second step requires a disputed self-locating update rule. The page explicitly forgoes that inference. 

That concession should propagate to every page that treats unitary phenomenology, witness consciousness or “finding oneself in one branch” as evidential support for a one-world ontology.

### One world does not remove self-location

As argued above, same-world duplicates reproduce the indexical problem. Tenet 4 may simplify the ontology of outcomes but does not explain subject pairing or why this subject is this one.

### Verdict

**Treat No Many Worlds as a chosen one-world boundary condition, not as a result derived from phenomenology. Make its dependence on determinate subjecthood and global exclusion explicit.**

---

## Tenet 5: Occam’s Razor Has Limits

### Coherent core

Parsimony is defeasible, theory-relative and not a substitute for explanatory adequacy. A simpler theory may omit the phenomenon needing explanation.

### Principal strain: the corpus sometimes uses a reverse razor

Several pages move from:

- simplicity is not decisive,

to:

- a more complex interface is therefore acceptable,

and then to:

- convergence or unification positively supports that interface.

The last step does not follow. Blocking a parsimony objection removes one defeater; it does not add likelihood evidence. The positions methodology explicitly recognizes this, while the latest tenet check still found “most parsimonious,” “strongly supports” and similar formulations in process, cross-traditional and clinical wings.  

Historical cases in which simplicity misled show that simple heuristics are fallible. They do not imply that complexity carries no prior cost.

### Verdict

**Keep Tenet 5, but operationalize it as a restraint on dismissal, never as positive evidence for the Map.**

# 5. Citation and empirical-claim audit

## 5.1 Born rule and no-signalling

**Citation status:** broadly accurate; inferential scope overstated.

The recent causal-consistency results are real and relevant. They strengthen the case that arbitrary nonlinear modifications of the Born probability map can enable signalling under steering. But they operate within specified generalized-probabilistic frameworks and assumptions. They do not establish the full psychophysical claim that every public intention-, subject- or task-conditioned neural ensemble must be exactly Born. [arXiv+1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.12636)

**Required correction:** distinguish:

- preservation of the unconditional local marginal;
- preservation under freely chosen measurement settings in an entangled scenario;
- preservation for every public classical label;
- and preservation conditional on inaccessible phenomenal variables.

These are different claims.

## 5.2 Decoherence

**Citation status:** the empirical physics is generally sound; the metaphysical role is overstated.

Fast environmental decoherence strongly constrains proposals requiring sustained neural quantum coherence. It makes post-decoherence approaches comparatively less exposed to that particular objection. It does not create a selection opportunity, establish absolute outcomes or show that consciousness acts at the decohered branching structure. The measurement-problem significance of decoherence remains interpretation-sensitive. [APS Journals](https://journals.aps.org/rmp/abstract/10.1103/RevModPhys.76.1267)

**Required correction:** describe post-decoherence selection as solving a timing incompatibility, not as supplying the missing interaction mechanism.

## 5.3 Avian magnetoreception and photosynthesis

**Citation status:** the Robin CRY4 work is genuine; several corpus formulations outrun it.

Xu and colleagues found that European robin cryptochrome 4 is magnetically sensitive in vitro and more sensitive than homologues from non-migratory birds. That is evidence for a plausible molecular ingredient. It is not a demonstration that the complete avian compass mechanism is quantum, still less that neural consciousness exploits related coherence. Contemporary summaries and later work continue to describe the radical-pair mechanism as plausible but not conclusively established at organismal scale. [Nature+2](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03618-9)

Photosynthetic energy transfer is even less useful as a bridge to consciousness unless the exact coherence type, duration and functional necessity are stated. Generic “biology uses quantum effects” language is nearly vacuous: all chemistry is quantum-mechanical.

**Required correction:** use magnetoreception only as a contested biological precedent for functionally relevant spin chemistry. Remove photosynthesis from any sentence claiming demonstrated, cognition-relevant quantum coherence.

## 5.4 Neurological and functional dissociations

**Citation status:** materially improved; remaining metaphysical inference is weak.

The current neurological-dissociation article now acknowledges:

- the degraded-conscious-vision interpretation of blindsight;
- the non-clean nature of pain-asymbolia lesions;
- belief-updating and attention contributions to anosognosia;
- predictive-processing interpretations of functional disorders;
- and the fact that a sophisticated functionalist predicts the same modular failure patterns.

Its final evidential grade—compatibility rather than vindication—is substantially more defensible. 

Predictive-processing and Bayesian accounts of functional neurological symptoms provide a concrete physical rival in which abnormal precision, priors and attention generate agency and symptom dissociations without an extracerebral interface. [DOI+1](https://doi.org/10.1093/BRAIN/AWS129)

**Residual defect:** dissociation identifies functional separability under assumptions about lesion transparency and locality. It does not identify which side of a metaphysical interface each function belongs to.

## 5.5 Readiness potentials and voluntary action

**Citation status:** simple Libet-style claims are outdated; newer Map revisions appear to recognize this.

Schurger and colleagues showed that the readiness potential can emerge from averaging spontaneous subthreshold fluctuations aligned to movement onset, rather than representing a settled unconscious decision made hundreds of milliseconds before action. [Chapman University+1](https://digitalcommons.chapman.edu/psychology_articles/175/)

That undermines a crude neuroscientific argument against freedom. It does not positively support nonphysical agency. Residual stochasticity, accumulator thresholds and late commitment are all compatible with physical models.

**Required correction:** every agency page should treat the Schurger result as removing one alleged defeater, never as evidence for Tenet 3.

## 5.6 AI consciousness

**Citation status:** the site’s low-probability verdict is framework-conditional, not an empirical consensus.

Butlin and colleagues derive indicator properties from multiple scientific theories of consciousness. They did not conclude that current systems are conscious, but neither did they identify an obvious technical barrier preventing future systems from satisfying the proposed indicators. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708)

IIT also cannot be filed simply as generic functionalism. IIT 4.0 makes distinctive claims about intrinsic causal structure and physical realization. A digital simulation need not instantiate the same intrinsic cause–effect structure as the system simulated. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1011465)

Conversely, recent no-go-style arguments against consciousness in contemporary chips are conditional on substantive premises such as dynamical relevance; they do not provide an architecture-independent proof. [LMU Open Access Fund](https://oa-fund.ub.uni-muenchen.de/id/eprint/1578/)

**Required correction:** separate three claims:

1. The Map’s own mechanism makes current conventional AI unlikely.
2. Several current scientific theories assign different probabilities.
3. Present evidence underdetermines the verdict.

Only the first follows directly from the tenets, and it depends on the missing eligibility law.

## 5.7 Diósi–Penrose collapse

**Citation status:** one of the better-calibrated areas.

The relevant experiment strongly constrains or rules out the natural parameter-free version tested, not every possible gravity-related objective-collapse model. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.13490)

The current register appears to preserve that scope rather than treating one experimental result as a refutation of all objective collapse. This should be the model for other empirical claims: specify the exact parameterization and theory version tested.

## 5.8 Evolution, valence and co-optimization

**Citation status:** the latest internal review identifies a serious source-use problem.

The co-optimization article presents a two-horn dilemma for epiphenomenalism but omits a reference-fixing reply discussed in its own cited source. It also counts the psychophysical harmony problem against epiphenomenalism while later Map work concedes that standard interactionism needs both inbound and outbound psychophysical laws. Its positive economy argument works only with an additional phenomenal-powers thesis, not with interactionism in general. 

This is a representative site-wide issue: the citation may be real and nearby prose may be accurate, while the article’s argument omits the source’s strongest reply or asks the evidence to discriminate more than it does.

# 6. What the site does particularly well

## 6.1 It increasingly distinguishes compatibility from discrimination

The current neurological-dissociation page is a strong example. It explicitly says that lesion, pharmacological and psychogenic routes may converge on a channel taxonomy without deciding whether those channels constitute consciousness or connect to it.

The voids register’s suspended P-V3 and empty rejection ledger are unusually candid. The observer-selection void explicitly blocks an inference that would otherwise favour Tenet 4. These are not perfunctory caveats; they genuinely reduce what the site may claim.  

## 6.4 It can absorb hostile review rapidly

The recent neurological-dissociation changes corrected overstatements about blindsight, pain asymbolia, attention, split-brain unity and pharmacological mechanisms within the article itself. 

The remaining challenge is to make those corrections propagate automatically.

## 6.5 It has developed a valuable distinction between evidence and constitutional choice

The multi-axis position schema allows a claim to be highly central but externally weak. That is exactly the right way to represent the quantum-interface and subjecthood commitments. The site’s best future form may be one in which the Map openly says:

- here are the chosen metaphysical boundary conditions;
- here are the independent empirical constraints;
- here are the framework-internal consequences;
- and here are the observations that would distinguish this package from its strongest rival.

That would be more credible than trying to make every clinical or phenomenological regularity into partial confirmation.
