## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

- **Evans's conditions half-stated (attribution).** The lead offered "either discriminating knowledge or an information-carrying causal channel", and the trade-off had Evans restore singular thought "through distinguishing facts". Hurry 2021 (the page's source for Evans) reads Evans as requiring *both* information derived from the object *and* discriminating knowledge, "individually necessary and jointly sufficient for thinking a singular thought". Fixed in three places: the Evans section gains one sentence on the information condition; the trade-off now reads "discriminating knowledge and an information link"; the lead reads "discriminating knowledge, an information-carrying causal channel, or both". The "no general phenomenal requirement" claim about Russell's Principle is unchanged and correct. No "neither is phenomenal" was added, because on Smithies's reading Evans holds consciousness essential for demonstrative thought.
- **Lewis quantifier inflated.** "Counted any causal chain…" stated a universal that the source lacks. Lewis's passage (Recanati 2009 p. 18 n. 4) lists letters, a biography, a name, crime-scene clues and says "I call such relations as these relations of acquaintance". Recanati frames it as a generalisation "in virtue of the analogy" with perceptual acquaintance. The sentence now reads "counted relations involving causal chains…".
- **(surname, year) orphans.** Grice (1969), Strawson (1974) and Perry (1980) were dated inline (the mental-file lineage, via Recanati 2009 p. 17) with no References entries. Strawson 1974 was hidden by the Strawson 1959 entry. All three were added after verification (ledger below).
- **Recanati attribution ambiguity.** "Recanati observes [intuitions] 'not particularly robust'… and argues that Hawthorne and Manley's report-based test… erases the singular/general distinction" reports Recanati's *New Essays* chapter (2010, via Ninan). The only Recanati entry in References is the 2009 *Manuscrito* paper, which never mentions Hawthorne or Manley (grep: 0 hits). The sentence now reads "Recanati, in *New Essays*, observes…". It stays undated, so no orphan is created.
- **Internal tension (Relation to Site Perspective, Dualism).** "It [this literature] shows where consciousness can enter: … on moderate PIT, through the thinker" conflicted with §Trade-off's "the singular-thought literature neither supplies nor threatens it". Rewritten word-neutrally: "Consciousness can enter narrowly, through acquaintance in demonstrative thought, and, on moderate PIT, held on other grounds, through the thinker in every case."

### Medium Issues Found

- **Bach qualifier.** "It must stand in some relation to that very thought" weakened Bach's "a certain kind of relation" (Recanati 2009 n. 7, p. 21). Restored.
- **Lead "Caesar or Neptune".** Evans and Bach do not grant pre-observation Le Verrier singular thought about Neptune. The disjunction is accurate as written (each listed view restores the Caesar case), so it was left alone. Flagged here in case a future edit turns "or" into "and".
