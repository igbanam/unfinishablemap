# Hostile-Referee Audit of The Unfinishable Map (live site, checked 15–20 October 2026)

The Map’s most original contribution is how it audits itself: the positions register, the “bias-without-deviation” dilemma, and the open admission that no toy model of its mechanism exists. That auditing has not reached the corpus as a whole. Older and secondary articles still overstate the quantum case, contradict the tenets page on the Zeno mechanism, decoherence numbers and quantum biology, and misdescribe at least three primary sources. The quantum-interface position, on the Map’s own Born-rule reading, also concedes the probabilistic form of causal closure that Kim and Papineau actually need.

## TL;DR

- **Novelty.** The real advances are methodological and diagnostic: the calibrated positions register (P-Q2, P-Q3, P-Q10), the “self-concealing interface” and conditioned-Born-deviation framing, the possibility/probability-slippage discipline, and parts of the voids catalogue. The argumentative core (zombies, Mary, Elitzur’s self-stultification, Stapp/Wigner quantum interactionism, Chisholm/Lowe/O’Connor agent causation, Schurger on Libet) restates Chalmers, Jackson, Popper–Eccles, Stapp and Swinburne without adding to them.
- **Coherence.** The tenets page is now careful. It concedes indistinguishability from chance “by construction” and ranks Stapp’s Zeno model behind post-decoherence selection. Many downstream pages have not caught up: they call Stapp “the most explicit implementation” of Tenet 2, cite photosynthetic coherence as live evidence even though another Map page notes it was overturned (Cao et al. 2020, *Science Advances*, report “the absence of long-lived interexciton coherence on relevant time scales”), give the Hagan et al. correction as seven versus eight-to-nine orders of magnitude, and argue from parsimony while Tenet 5 disarms parsimony.
- **Citations and steelmen.** I found real errors: Schurger’s “Libetus interruptus” task is misdescribed, Maoz et al.’s arbitrary condition is miscast as “donation choices”, Proietti et al. 2019 is presented as empirically “confirming” Frauchiger–Renner contradictions, and Frankish is said to predict that the illusion should “dissolve” (he says the opposite). The replies to illusionism and causal closure are weaker than the Map believes.

## Key Findings

### 1. Novelty: what adds something and what recapitulates

**Genuinely novel or distinctive (keep and foreground):**

- **The positions register and mechanism-debt convention.** Tenets now concede that P-Q3 “sits genuinely close to epiphenomenalism” and that P-Q10 records “no worked toy model of the coupling exists” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) I know of no comparable public dualist project that grades its own central mechanism “framework-internal only”. This is the site’s best feature.
- **The scoped indistinguishability claim.** Selection is “empirically indistinguishable from chance… by construction”, but “a deviation *conditioned* on intention, task or subject would test the corridor itself” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) Separating unconditioned from conditioned Born deviations is a sharp, testable reframing of the old “psi-free interactionism” problem. It is more precise than Stapp’s or Eccles’s treatments.
- **Tenet 5’s symmetric self-binding.** The Map declines to use parsimony against Many-Worlds because it disarms parsimony against dualism (“the Map cannot invoke simplicity against many-worlds while disarming parsimony arguments *against* dualism”, [https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) This is principled and uncommon.
- **Possibility/probability slippage.** “A tenet may remove a defeater, but it must not upgrade the evidence level” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/possibility-probability-slippage/](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/possibility-probability-slippage/)) is a useful methodological contribution. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/possibility-probability-slippage/)
- **Some voids.** The 10-15 impossible-percept research note’s observation that impossible percepts invert the Map’s own “refer-but-not-grasp” asymmetry (“grasp-but-not-refer”) is a real conceptual move ([https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/workflow/changelog/)

**Recapitulations (competent but not new):**

- **Against Materialism** ([https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/materialism-argument/](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/materialism-argument/)) is a textbook assembly: the hard problem (Chalmers), zombies, Mary (Jackson), Elitzur’s argument, the von Neumann–Wigner–Stapp route through collapse, and the anti-Occam move. Its own description of Stapp (“consciousness affects brain states by biasing quantum probabilities through the quantum Zeno effect”) adds nothing to Stapp, [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/materialism-argument/) and it does not engage Popper–Eccles (1977) or Beck–Eccles (1992), the closest historical precedents for “biasing quantum probabilities without energy injection”.
- **Agent Causation** ([https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/agent-causation/](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/agent-causation/)) is a survey of Reid, Chisholm, O’Connor, Lowe and Hasker. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/agent-causation/) The Map’s claimed addition (“What the Map adds is mechanism”) is exactly the Eccles/Stapp move, now placed under P-Q10’s admission that no worked model exists. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/agent-causation/) The addition is a promissory note, not a contribution.
- **The Libet cluster** (libet-experiments; volitional-control; phenomenology-of-choice-and-volition; free-will; attention-and-the-consciousness-interface) retells Schurger 2012, Maoz 2019, Sjöberg 2024 and Desmurget 2009 at least five times. The only distinctive element is the retrocausal and “atemporal selection” gloss, which is speculative and pulls against Tenet 2 (see §3).
- **Post-decoherence selection** is conceptually the “problem of outcomes” (Schlosshauer) plus a conscious selector. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns/) Its novelty lies in the careful hedging, not the idea.

### 3. Tenet coherence: where articles strain against the tenets

**(a) Tenet 2 versus the Zeno and retrocausal pages.** The tenets page classes Stapp’s Zeno model among proposals that “depend on pre-decoherence coherence”, ranked behind post-decoherence selection, and explicitly does not adopt Stapp’s Process-1 (context-selection) placement (“the Map has registered this alternative but *not* adopted it”, [https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) Yet:

- testing-consciousness-collapse says Stapp’s Zeno approach “aligns with the Map’s Minimal Quantum Interaction tenet” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/testing-consciousness-collapse/](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/testing-consciousness-collapse/)); [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/testing-consciousness-collapse/)
- consciousness-in-smeared-quantum-states (search-index snippet) calls “Stapp’s model… the most explicit implementation of the Map’s tenet”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/consciousness-in-smeared-quantum-states/)

On the minimality side, the Libet page’s retrocausal proposal has consciousness select “which temporal sequence becomes real—including the RP that ‘preceded’ it” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/libet-experiments/](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/libet-experiments/)), [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/libet-experiments/) and Agent Causation answers the luck objection with “if selection is atemporal, the prior state is part of what consciousness selected” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/agent-causation/](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/agent-causation/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/agent-causation/) Selecting whole neural histories, including prior states, is not “the smallest deviation from standard quantum mechanics that could permit mind-matter causation” (Tenet 2’s definition). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) It is the largest-scope influence anywhere on the site.

**(b) Tenet 5 versus parsimony arguments in the Map’s favour.**

- Consciousness-Selecting Neural Patterns prefers forward-in-time selection because it “carries fewer commitments… (temporal parsimony)” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns/](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns/)
- Retrocausality prefers Schurger-type resolutions that “require less interpretational commitment” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/retrocausality/](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/retrocausality/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/retrocausality/)

These are ordinary uses of Occam’s razor. The tenets page’s defence that Tenet 2’s minimality is “empirical-constraint minimality” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)) protects Tenet 2 only. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) It does not cover intra-framework choices between mechanisms, which are pure simplicity preferences. The site must either license parsimony for adjudicating within the framework (and say why it is reliable there but not across frameworks) or stop using it.

**(c) Tenet 4 is under-specified for the work it does.** Consciousness-Selecting Neural Patterns concedes that “Everettian branching, Bohmian hidden variables, objective-collapse dynamics, and pragmatist quietism each dissolve the ‘problem of outcomes’” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns/](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns/) Only the first is excluded by a tenet. The mechanism needs an indeterministic, single-outcome, non-Bohmian, non-relational ontology. Retrocausality also leans on an “objective collapse” growing block and on Cramer/Kastner’s transactional interpretation ([https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/retrocausality/](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/retrocausality/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/retrocausality/) A reader cannot tell which interpretations of quantum mechanics are actually ruled out.

testing-consciousness-collapse also cites Proietti et al. 2019 as support, but that paper’s conclusion is that, under locality and free choice, “quantum theory should be interpreted in an observer-dependent way”. That conclusion sits uneasily with the tenets’ “insistence on global uniqueness” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)[harvard](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2019SciA....5.9832P)

**(d) Causal closure is rejected in the tenets but quietly conceded downstream.**

- Tenet 2 says: “Physics is *not* complete at quantum indeterminacies—and that’s where consciousness acts” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)
- Agent Causation offers trumping preemption: “even granting physical sufficiency, the agent can be the *authoritative* cause” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/agent-causation/](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/agent-causation/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/agent-causation/)
- The Free Will topic concedes that “Born-preserving selection leaves probabilistic closure intact” (search-index snippet, [https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/free-will/](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/free-will/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/free-will/)

These are three different strategies with different costs, and the last undercuts the first (see the steelman in §6).

### 5. Citation and quotation accuracy (sample)

- **Schurger, Sitt & Dehaene 2012: misdescribed.** The Libet page says the “‘Libetus interruptus’ experiment—interrupting participants randomly and asking if they felt an urge—found that subjective urges correlated with neural fluctuation levels” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/libet-experiments/](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/libet-experiments/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/libet-experiments/) In the actual task, a click cues subjects “to make the movement immediately, as quickly as possible”. [researchgate](https://www.researchgate.net/figure/Libetus-Interruptus-experiment-A-Premovement-potential-from-the-same-electrode_fig4_230624040) The confirmed prediction was that “Fast responses to temporally unpredictable interruptions should be preceded by a slow negative-going voltage deflection beginning well before the interruption itself” (PNAS abstract, [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22869750/](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22869750/)). [nih](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22869750/) Urges were not probed. **Correction needed.**
- **Maoz et al. 2019: partly misdescribed.** The phrase “strikingly absent” is verbatim in the abstract (“While we found the expected RPs for arbitrary decisions, they were strikingly absent for deliberate ones”). [iucc](https://cris.iucc.ac.il/en/publications/neural-precursors-of-decisions-that-matteran-erp-study-of-deliber/) But the Libet page’s “RPs for arbitrary donation choices” is misleading: in arbitrary trials “both NPOs received an equal donation of $500, irrespective of subjects’ key presses”. [elifesciences](https://elifesciences.org/articles/39787) The body text also hedges to “absent, or at least significantly reduced”. [elifesciences](https://elifesciences.org/articles/39787) The Libet page’s “Maoz et al. (2019) found no RP for deliberate choices” overstates this. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/libet-experiments/)

But “inhibition” here was a brief delay inferred from slower reaction times, with n = 13. [plos](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article) The page’s later caveat (“for rapid-versus-delayed responding rather than full cancellation”) is correct and should move up into the main description. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/libet-experiments/)

[researchgate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/301914085_Beyond_the_point_of_no_return_Last-minute_changes_in_human_motor_performance)

[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/testing-consciousness-collapse/) But the paper says “The related no-go theorem by Frauchiger and Renner rests on different assumptions”, and its result is conditional: “If one holds fast to the assumptions of locality and free-choice, this result implies that quantum theory should be interpreted in an observer-dependent way” ([https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2019SciA....5.9832P](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2019SciA....5.9832P)). The experiment does not confirm a contradiction between universal quantum mechanics and *conscious* experience.

[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/research/steelman-for-value-blind-selection-2026-06-18/)[achrafkassioui](https://www.achrafkassioui.com/library/Keith%20Frankish%20-%20Illusionism%20as%20a%20Theory%20of%20Consciousness.pdf) The Illusionism page’s “eliminativism… he rejects the label” is fair (Frankish: “We might call this eliminativism about phenomenal consciousness. The term is not ideal…”). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/illusionism/)[achrafkassioui](https://www.achrafkassioui.com/library/Keith%20Frankish%20-%20Illusionism%20as%20a%20Theory%20of%20Consciousness.pdf) But Against Materialism argues: “If Frankish is right, phenomenal seeming should dissolve under sustained attention… it does not.” [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/materialism-argument/) The Map’s own research note records Frankish holding that the illusion “is ‘rich’—it persists regardless of what one comes to believe about its reality” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/research/illusionism-functional-seeming-2026-03-28/](https://unfinishablemap.org/research/illusionism-functional-seeming-2026-03-28/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/research/illusionism-functional-seeming-2026-03-28/) The Map is attributing to Frankish a prediction he explicitly denies; Müller-Lyer-type persistence is his model case. [achrafkassioui](https://www.achrafkassioui.com/library/Keith%20Frankish%20-%20Illusionism%20as%20a%20Theory%20of%20Consciousness.pdf)
- **Tegmark 2000: correct where stated in full.** The tenets page’s “10⁻²⁰ seconds for neuron firing, 10⁻¹³ for microtubules” matches the abstract. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)[arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9907009) The “femtosecond” glosses do not.
- **Wheeler delayed choice: overstated.** Retrocausality says experiments “confirm that measurement choices made *after* a particle passes… can determine whether it behaved as wave or particle *earlier*” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/retrocausality/](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/retrocausality/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/retrocausality/) That is an interpretation, not an experimental finding. In the canonical test, Jacques, Wu, Grosshans, Treussart, Grangier, Aspect & Roch (*Science* 315:966, 2007), the open/closed choice was made by a quantum random number generator space-like separated from the photon’s entry. The paper reports interference correlations, not retro-determination. Ma, Kofler & Zeilinger (*Rev. Mod. Phys.* 88, 015005, 2016) describe such experiments as aiming to exclude photons “a priori adapting to the specific experimental situation”. The same page’s claim that “Retrocausality has serious proponents and empirical grounding in physics” conflates the two. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/retrocausality/)

- Agent Causation’s “Kane’s 2024 work now incorporates agent-causal elements” needs a page citation. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/agent-causation/) Kane has long defended event-causal libertarianism.
- “Cross-cultural convergence” (Nyāya, Akan) is offered as evidence for agent causation. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/agent-causation/) Convergent folk metaphysics is evidence about cognition, not about causal structure.

### 6. Steelmen the Map answers too quickly

**Papineau/Kim, probabilistic closure.** Papineau’s mature closure principle is probabilistic. In “The Causal Closure of the Physical and Naturalism” (*Oxford Handbook of Philosophy of Mind*, 2009) he writes that “quantum mechanics still specifies that random physical effects have their probabilities fixed by sufficient immediate physical causes”, and that any sui generis cause “would have to make a difference to the probability of the relevant physical effect”. The Map’s endorsed reading (P-Q2) preserves Born statistics exactly, and the Free Will topic concedes that “Born-preserving selection leaves probabilistic closure intact”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/free-will/) So the closure premise the physicalist needs is granted. The remaining “selection” is a token-level fact with no difference to any physical chance. That is precisely Kim’s exclusion picture, with the mental as an idle actualiser. Calling it “trumping preemption” does not help: trumping requires that the trumping cause make a counterfactual difference somewhere, and the Map has ruled that out for every unconditioned test. P-Q3’s admission that the selection “sits genuinely close to epiphenomenalism” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)) is the honest statement. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/) The articles that say quantum mechanics “is not causally closed in the way materialism requires” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/materialism-argument/](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/materialism-argument/)) should be rewritten to match it. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/)

**Tegmark, properly read.** Tegmark’s conclusion is that cognitively relevant degrees of freedom are classical: “there is nothing fundamentally wrong with the current classical approach to neural network simulations” ([https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9907009](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9907009)). [arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9907009) Post-decoherence selection does not “sidestep” this, as testing-consciousness-collapse claims. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/topics/testing-consciousness-collapse/) It concedes it, then adds an unobservable actualisation step on top of a classical dynamics that already fully explains behaviour. The strongest form of the objection is that the Map’s endorsed mechanism is explanatorily idle for neuroscience. The Map’s best reply is P-Q10’s “no toy model”, which is an admission, not a rebuttal.

**Frankish and Dennett.** The Map’s two anti-illusionist arguments are:

- “the representing state and the represented state coincide”, so no independent vantage exists;
- the illusion should dissolve under attention. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/materialism-argument/)

The second misreads Frankish (see §5). The first assumes what is at issue: illusionists hold that introspective self-models are ordinary representations that can be wrong about their own vehicles, as any self-monitoring system can. The strongest version is a predictive-processing self-model. Its interoceptive and attentional priors predict both the persistence of the seeming and its resistance to theoretical knowledge. Two examples are Graziano’s attention schema and the 2021 *Neuroscience of Consciousness* model by Sandved-Smith, Hesp, Mattout, Friston, Lutz & Ramstead, which casts “mental action as policy selection over higher-level cognitive states”. The Map acknowledges active inference models “the bandwidth bottleneck, the felt cost of holding attention, and the coarse-grained allocation of detail… without invoking anything non-physical” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns/](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns/)) but still lists “the phenomenology of effort persists” as a reply to illusionism. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/consciousness-selecting-neural-patterns/) Effort phenomenology is exactly what that model predicts.

**Self-stultification against illusionism.** The Libet page claims “The illusionist cannot coherently claim to have *reasoned* to their position” ([https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/libet-experiments/](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/libet-experiments/)). [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/libet-experiments/) Illusionism denies phenomenal properties, not reasoning. Inference is a functional and representational process the illusionist fully retains. The tenets page itself concedes that the phenomenal-concept strategy survives self-stultification and that illusionism must be met “at a framework boundary”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/tenets/)[unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/materialism-argument/) The Libet page should not claim a refutation the tenets page disowns.

**Churchland and the “dissolution test”.** Against Materialism argues that contemplative training “should dissolve the seeming the way scientific instruction dissolved phlogiston-talk”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/materialism-argument/) Churchland’s reply is that perceptual and introspective categories are retrained by theory-laden practice, not by verbal instruction. Long-term meditators’ reports do shift: the Map’s own text concedes “the tradition’s own analysis of *vedanā* points toward *anattā*”. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/arguments/materialism-argument/) The evidence the Map cites is therefore at best neutral.
