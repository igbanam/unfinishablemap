# Referee Report: Audit of "Agent Causation" (Ref: The Unfinishable Map)

## 1. Introduction and Architectural Breakdown of the Manuscript

The manuscript under review, located within the broader project of *The Unfinishable Map*, advances an ambitious but fundamentally flawed defense of non-reductive, agent-causal libertarian free will. The text attempts to forge a synthesis across disparate intellectual domains, grounding a hyper-traditionalist metaphysical commitment—interactionist substance dualism—in selective interpretations of contemporary neuroscience, hierarchical motor control, and quantum mechanics. The manuscript specifically formalizes its position via two registered commitments: P-A1 (agent-causal libertarian free will) and P-A2 (the assertion that genuine authorship requires substance-based agent causation rather than mere event-causal indeterminism). To operationalize these commitments in the physical world, the author posits a "Mind-Matter Interface," hypothesizing that a non-physical conscious agent intervenes in brain activity through "minimal quantum selection," putatively exploiting the Quantum Zeno Effect (QZE) to bypass the causal closure of classical physics.

While the architectural ambition of synthesizing metaphysics, neurobiology, and quantum mechanics is evident, a rigorous audit of the manuscript’s logical inferences, counterargument coverage, and empirical citations reveals a project that is systematically decoupled from the vanguard of 2020–2025 academic literature. The manuscript does not participate in the modern scientific or philosophical consensus; instead, it aggressively retrofits anachronistic metaphysical frameworks onto a highly distorted, cherry-picked selection of empirical data.

The ensuing report details the catastrophic structural failures of the manuscript across four primary vectors of analysis. First, the metaphysical scaffolding relies on a strawman caricature of event-causal libertarianism, intentionally ignoring the sophisticated hybrid dynamical models developed in the contemporary literature, thereby allowing the author to illegitimately bracket the luck objection by linguistic fiat. Second, the manuscript’s foundational neuroscientific "live test"—relying on the absence of the readiness potential (RP) in deliberate decisions—is predicated on an obsolete, deterministic interpretation of the RP that has been thoroughly falsified by stochastic accumulator models in the 2024–2025 literature. Third, the text's conflation of selective attention with motor execution demonstrates a profound ignorance of hierarchical predictive processing, specifically omitting forward models and efference copies. Finally, the proposed mechanism of "post-decoherence quantum selection" rests on a demonstrable misunderstanding of density matrices, rendering the hypothesis physically impossible under known quantum mechanics.

## 2. Metaphysical Deficits: The Evasion of Modern Action Theory

The philosophical core of the manuscript operates on a rigid, archaic bifurcation between "transeunt" (event) causation and "immanent" (substance/agent) causation, relying almost entirely on the historical taxonomies provided by Reid (1788), Chisholm (1964), and Lowe (2008). The manuscript asserts that event-causal libertarianism—the position that free actions are caused by indeterministic mental events—inevitably fails to secure genuine authorship. The text invokes van Inwagen’s Consequence Argument, claiming that if an action is the result of prior mental events, and the agent did not choose those prior events, the causal chain traces back beyond the agent's control, thus rendering the "direction of fit" entirely metaphorical. The manuscript subsequently posits its substance-dualist agent causation as the sole viable resolution to the "trilemma of selection" (the choice between determinism, randomness, and mental event causation).

### 2.1 The Strawman of Event-Causal Libertarianism and the AC/EC Synthesis

This framing is an egregious misrepresentation of the contemporary philosophy of action. The manuscript dismisses event-causal libertarianism using objections formulated in the late 20th century, completely failing to engage with modern dynamical systems theory and hybrid models that successfully resolve the "disappearing agent" objection without resorting to the ontological bloat of substance dualism.

The manuscript’s superficial engagement with Robert Kane’s 2024 work, *The Complex Tapestry of Free Will: A Philosophical Odyssey*, exemplifies this deficit. The text notes in passing that "Kane's 2024 work now incorporates agent-causal elements", but aggressively misinterprets the implications of this theoretical evolution. Kane does not retreat into the substance dualism demanded by the manuscript. Instead, Kane formulates an Agent-Causal/Event-Causal (AC/EC) synthesis, introducing the mechanism of "Teleological Guidance Control" (TGC). Under this modern framework, agents are treated as information-responsive, complex dynamical systems that exercise guidance control over their own cognitive processes. Kane demonstrates that the agent can function as an efficient cause (in the Aristotelian sense) by establishing formal and final constraints on indeterministic neural noise.

By framing the agent as a macroscopic dynamical constraint rather than a detached, non-physical substance, contemporary models like Kane's AC/EC synthesis achieve exactly the genuine origination and "directedness" the manuscript claims are exclusive to substance dualism. The manuscript's assertion that event-causal accounts cannot ground authorship is not a reasoned philosophical argument; it is a dogmatic refusal to acknowledge the last half-decade of literature on teleological emergence and dynamic systems theory. The manuscript forces a false dichotomy, assuming that if one rejects classical event-causal reductionism, one must inevitably embrace a persisting, non-physical Cartesian-adjacent substance.

### 2.2 The Vacuity of the Defense Against the Luck Objection

Because the manuscript relies on an artificially inflated necessity for substance causation, its defense against standard anti-libertarian defeaters—most notably, the cross-world luck objection—is purely nominalistic. The cross-world luck objection posits a scenario where Agent S in World W1 decides to undertake Action A, while an identical Agent S in World W2, subject to the exact same history, reasons, laws of physics, and neural states up to the moment of choice, decides to undertake Action B. If absolutely nothing about the agent or the prior universe accounts for the difference in outcome, the choice is ultimately governed by luck or randomness, which annihilates moral responsibility.

The manuscript attempts to bracket this devastating objection through linguistic fiat. It invokes the agent as an "uncaused cause," asserting that the regress simply stops at the agent's "irreducible capacity to act". This is an abdication of explanatory duty, not a philosophical resolution. Merely naming the locus of the indeterministic gap "the agent" does not explain the mechanics of rational control across divergent possible worlds. David Palmer’s (2022) comprehensive analysis of libertarianism and luck in the *Journal of Philosophical Theological Research* demonstrates that merely inserting a dualistic substance into the causal chain does not magically inoculate the framework against arbitrariness. The metaphysical chasm between the agent's reasons and the eventual indeterminate selection remains highly vulnerable to the luck objection.

Furthermore, the manuscript's changelog indicates recent frantic patching of the "Causal Consistency Constraint" and the integration of Siegel's perceptual thesis of causation, alongside citations to Gandevia et al. (2006) on the introspection of outgoing motor commands in paralytics. The author attempts to argue that because we possess a subjective asymmetry in perceiving outgoing motor commands versus incoming sensory data, we must therefore possess genuine, irreducible agent-causal efficacy. This is a severe category error. Introspective asymmetry only proves that the brain processes efferent and afferent signals differently; it provides absolutely zero epistemic justification for the existence of a non-physical substance bypassing causal closure. The phenomenology of intellectual effort and the perception of volition do not map one-to-one onto metaphysical reality. By relying on phenomenological perception to validate a highly contested ontology, the manuscript engages in circular reasoning, shielding its foundational commitments from objective metaphysical scrutiny.

## 3. Neuroscientific Obsolescence: The Readiness Potential Paradigm Shift

The manuscript’s attempt to ground its metaphysical speculations in empirical neuroscience rests structurally on a catastrophic misreading of the readiness potential (RP). The author cites the highly publicized finding by Maoz et al. (2019) that the RP is "strikingly absent" in deliberate decisions (e.g., choosing to donate to a charity), in stark contrast to arbitrary decisions (e.g., the classic Libet wrist-flexing paradigm). The manuscript frames this absence as a literal physical gap in the causal chain, a "live test" where deterministic neural prediction fails, thereby leaving an empty space for the non-physical agent to initiate causal action.

### 3.1 The Fundamental Mischaracterization of the Readiness Potential

The manuscript treats the RP as a deterministic biomarker of impending action—a neural signal of "motor preparation" that, if present, threatens free will, and if absent, saves it. This foundational premise has been entirely dismantled by the computational and neuroscientific vanguard of the 2020s, spearheaded by the very researchers the manuscript attempts to weaponize.

Between 2020 and 2025, the study of volition underwent a massive computational revision. Gavenas, Schurger, and Maoz (2024), publishing in *Nature Communications*, explicitly falsified the interpretation of the RP as a top-down, causal preparatory signal. Through rigorous computational modeling, they demonstrated that the slow ramping of the RP is actually an artifact of spontaneous fluctuations in spiking neural networks—a stochastic drift-diffusion process. In arbitrary tasks where there is no external cue to prompt an action, the brain relies on autocorrelated internal neural noise. When this noise randomly drifts across a specific threshold, movement is triggered. When researchers time-lock their EEG averaging to the movement, they naturally capture the upward slope of the noise that crossed the threshold, creating the illusion of a deliberate, ramping preparatory signal. The RP is thus a statistical reflection of temporal accumulation of noise, not a deterministic causal driver of intention.

### 3.2 The Collapse of the "Live Test" for Agent Causation

Because the manuscript fundamentally misconstrues the ontological nature of the RP, its entire analysis of deliberate decisions collapses. The absence of the RP in deliberate decisions (as documented by Maoz 2019, and corroborated by Pech et al. 2025) does not indicate a metaphysical "gap" where a dualistic agent assumes control. Rather, it simply indicates a shift in the mechanism of evidence accumulation. In a deliberate decision, the agent is weighing external semantic, moral, or practical variables. The action is cue-locked to the resolution of this cognitive evaluation, rather than triggered by the stochastic crossing of internal neural noise. Because the trigger mechanism is different, time-locking the EEG backward from the movement does not reveal the classic RP slope.

By clinging to a pre-2020 understanding of the RP as a threat to free will that must be circumvented, the manuscript completely misses the modern consensus: the RP is causally inert concerning the phenomenological experience of agency. The manuscript's assertion that 100% neural prediction would "leave no gap" and that current inaccuracy validates agent causation is a classical "God of the gaps" fallacy applied to neurobiology. The data the author uses to argue against event-causal physicalism actually provides the strongest empirical support for it, seamlessly mapping decision-making onto stochastic accumulator models.

### 3.3 Falsification of Phenomenological Coupling

Furthermore, the manuscript relies heavily on the assumption that the neural signals preceding an action are tightly coupled to the agent's subjective experience of initiation. This, too, has been explicitly falsified. Gavenas, Schurger, and Maoz (2025), in a landmark study published in *Imaging Neuroscience* titled "Probing for intentions: The early readiness potential does not reflect awareness of motor preparation," deployed a real-time probe paradigm to test this exact coupling. They demonstrated that pre-probe EEG amplitude does not covary with participants' subsequent subjective reports of movement preparation. The early RP operates independently of subjective awareness. The manuscript’s reliance on "phenomenology" and "introspective asymmetry" to validate its model of conscious initiation is empirically bankrupt in light of this data.

Additionally, attempts to find novel physiological couplings to the RP have similarly failed. Jeay-Bizot et al. (2026), publishing in *PLoS Biology*, investigated claims that the RP is modulated by respiratory phases, demonstrating through Bayesian analysis that such apparent couplings were statistical artifacts of binning procedures, finding conclusive evidence for *no* coupling between respiratory phase and RP amplitude. The contemporary neuroscientific landscape is characterized by stripping away the mystical and causal weight previously attributed to pre-movement neural activity; the manuscript, conversely, builds an entire ontology upon it.

## 4. The Conflation of Attention and Action: Ignorance of Motor Control Hierarchies

In attempting to locate the specific physical "interface" where the non-physical agent exercises its quantum selection, the manuscript turns to the convergence of attention and motor planning. Citing Rizzolatti’s premotor theory of attention (PMTA) and Fine and Hayden’s (2022) assertion that the prefrontal cortex can be viewed as fundamentally premotor, the text argues that "selecting what to perceive may already be selecting what to do—one interface rather than two". The author leverages this purported unitary interface to argue that a singular, conscious agent can simultaneously manipulate dopamine release, visuospatial cueing, and motor commitment.

### 4.1 The Omission of Forward Models and Efference Copies

This highly reductionist, localized framework willfully ignores the standard, empirically dominant models of hierarchical motor control. The manuscript attempts to compress the vast, distributed, and functionally distinct architecture of action execution into a single metaphysical needle-point of "quantum selection" governed by conscious attention. In doing so, the author entirely omits the well-documented physiological mechanisms of "forward models" and "efference copies".

Contemporary cognitive neuroscience and philosophy of action—such as the work of Joshua Shepherd (2021) on central coordinating agency—demonstrate that motor representations are highly intelligent, flexible, and operate autonomously downstream of conscious intention. When an agent intends an action, the motor system translates this high-level semantic command into a specific kinetic sequence. Simultaneously, it generates an efference copy—a neural prediction of the sensory consequences of the impending movement. This efference copy is fed into a forward model, which continuously compares predicted sensory feedback against actual afferent feedback, allowing for rapid, unconscious error correction on millisecond timescales.

The presence of robust, unconscious predictive forward models destroys the manuscript's central premise of a unitary, conscious "attention-action" interface. If motor execution requires continuous, hierarchical, and largely unconscious predictive processing to function, the notion that a non-physical agent exercises "minimal quantum selection" at a singular convergence point becomes conceptually incoherent. The manuscript fails to specify the localization of its hypothesized quantum selection within the forward-model hierarchy. Does the agent select the high-level semantic intention? Or does the agent micromanage the descending motor commands at the level of the spinal cord? The literature on efference copies proves that intelligence and selective control exist within the motor system completely independently of conscious attention. By ignoring this massive body of biomechanical and neurological literature, the manuscript props up a scientifically illiterate model of homuncular agency.

### 4.2 The Metaphysical Fallacy of the "Choking" Phenomenon

The manuscript's reliance on empirical anomalies like "placebo effects" and "choking under pressure" to establish the causal efficacy of consciousness is similarly flawed, resting on a fundamental category error. The manuscript argues that choking—where a shift in self-focused attention degrades procedural execution in athletes or musicians—constitutes definitive, non-verbal evidence that consciousness exerts a causal, downward force on physical brain states.

This argument is an egregious non-sequitur. The degradation of motor performance under explicit, self-focused attention is perfectly modeled and predicted by strictly physicalist predictive processing frameworks. When higher-order cognitive networks (associated with explicit rule monitoring, working memory, and conscious attention) attempt to intervene in the highly optimized, automated unconscious forward models of the cerebellum and motor cortex, the resulting interference causes execution to become fragmented and clumsy. The manuscript makes an entirely unfounded metaphysical leap: it assumes that because the *cognitive state* of attention disrupts performance, an irreducible, non-physical substance must be the entity doing the disrupting. This completely ignores the fact that the neural correlates of attention and explicit monitoring are themselves physical events capable of transeunt (event) causation. The "choking" phenomenon does absolutely no unique explanatory work for agent causation; it merely demonstrates that differing physical networks in the brain can interfere with one another.

## 5. Quantum Mysticism: The Contradiction of the Macroscopic Zeno Interface

Perhaps the most scientifically indefensible aspect of the manuscript is its reliance on "quantum indeterminacies" and specifically the Quantum Zeno Effect (QZE) as the proposed mechanism for agent-causal selection. The text claims that consciousness can "select" or "freeze" neural states by acting as a quantum observer. Crucially, the text's changelog and internal review documents explicitly attempt to defend this by adopting a "post-decoherence selection" framework, arguing that the quantum Zeno mechanism can somehow operate after environmental decoherence has occurred.

### 5.1 The Mathematical Incoherence of "Post-Decoherence Selection"

The manuscript’s treatment of quantum mechanics relies on a profound, mathematical misunderstanding of environmental decoherence, the reduced density matrix, and biological systems. The Quantum Zeno Effect—where frequent measurement inhibits the unitary Hamiltonian evolution of a quantum state—is rigorously dependent on the continuous preservation of off-diagonal phase coherence within a superposition.

The brain, however, is a macroscopic, thermodynamic environment ("warm and wet"). Environmental decoherence—the rapid entanglement of a quantum system with the countless degrees of freedom in its thermal environment—diagonalizes the reduced density matrix at timescales estimated to be on the order of 10⁻¹³ seconds or faster. The manuscript paradoxically asserts a "post-decoherence selection" framework while simultaneously invoking the Quantum Zeno Effect to freeze macroscopic neural states. This is physically contradictory.

Once a system has undergone environmental decoherence, the off-diagonal phase coherence is destroyed, and the system is mathematically reduced to a classical statistical mixture of states. The Quantum Zeno Effect operates exclusively on coherent quantum superpositions. It is mathematically and physically impossible to apply a Zeno freeze to a post-decoherence classical probability distribution.

### 5.2 Definitively Closed Loopholes in the 2020–2025 Physics Literature

The contemporary physics literature systematically dismantles any remaining possibility of exploiting the QZE for macroscopic cognitive control. The manuscript's attempt to use "biological quantum precedents" (such as avian magnetoreception or photosynthetic efficiency) is a false equivalence. Those biological precedents rely on highly localized, femtosecond-scale mechanisms (e.g., radical pair mechanisms in specific proteins) that are heavily shielded from the broader environment. They do not involve macroscopic, brain-wide neural synchrony governed by a non-physical agent.

Recent studies on quantum transport and stabilization in highly controlled molecular environments decisively rule out the manuscript's hypothesis. Sarr et al. (2025/2026), publishing in the *Journal of Computational Chemistry*, specifically modeled the limits of the Quantum Zeno Effect in chemical systems under dephasing conditions. They proved that for moderate to strong dephasing environments—which are orders of magnitude less hostile than the dense synaptic environment of the human brain—the optimization law of the Quantum Zeno Effect is entirely lost. Similarly, investigations into macroscopic quantum effects, such as Chiari et al. (2025) in *Physical Review A*, highlight the extreme limitations of quantum transport across spin states without rigorous microscopic isolation.

The manuscript completely fails to provide a mathematically coherent interaction Hamiltonian that could explain how a non-physical substance couples to a physical neural network to induce a Zeno collapse without brutally violating the unitarity of physical conservation laws. Consequently, the proposed "Mind-Matter Interface" is reduced to quantum mysticism dressed in the vocabulary of philosophical metaphysics, offering no explanatory power and contradicting fundamental physics.
