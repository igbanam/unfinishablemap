# Hostile-Referee Audit: "The Self Is Constructed; the Constructor Is Not" (apex/self-construction-constructor)

**Headline verdict: DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY.** The "constructor inference" survives only in one of two forms. In the first, "constructor" means a functional system, and the claim is true but trivial. In the second, "constructor" means a phenomenal subject, and the claim simply restates Tenet 1 (phenomenal realism plus irreducibility). The article never secures a third reading in which construction is shown to be *unable* to generate the subject. Its own Evidence section half-concedes this. Its title, synthesis and tenet sections then reassert the claim unconditionally.

## Part 1 — TL;DR

- **Verdict: DEMOTE-TO-COHERENCE-ONLY.** The title and synthesis also need REVISE-HARD. The argument rests on one premise: "content requires a subject for whom it is content." Applied four times, that premise is presented as a "recurrence… across independent domains." It is one premise, not four pieces of evidence. The premise is exactly what self-model theory, Dennett's multiple-drafts account, illusionism and active-inference selfhood deny. The article also contradicts that premise itself: it grants that ape mindreading and the generative model's priors are content-bearing constructions that "need not be conscious."
- **Rival scorecard.** Metzinger is engaged but strawmanned: the "no one who is fooled" dilemma misstates SMT, and the site's own concept page calls Metzinger's reading "internally consistent and not refutable by introspective data." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/concepts/self-and-self-consciousness/) Illusionism is held off only by stipulation, and the article concedes in-text that illusionists deny the key premise. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-construction-constructor/) Dennett's narrative centre of gravity, the gap the brief names as (b), is **unanswered** in the apex. Predictive processing is **unanswered**, and the Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria 2025 blocking gate **fails**. The article never mentions "A beautiful loop" (*Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* 176:106296). [scu](https://researchportal.scu.edu.au/view/pdfCoverPage) That paper models the reflexive, self-knowing structure of experience as generated by recursive hierarchical inference. [osf](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/daf5n) It turns "the constructor cannot be generated" into "the hard problem remains," which is just Tenet 1 again.
- **Integration and citations.** The apex's handling of Kant's Paralogisms and IEM is accurate: it explicitly refuses to infer a substantial subject from the "I think" or from immunity to error. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-construction-constructor/) This is the article's most disciplined passage. But the caveats are confined to one paragraph. The Synthesis, Occam's-Razor and No-Many-Worlds sections go on to assert that the subject "cannot be the output of building," which is confession-without-correction. The apex has no reference list. ### (a) Argument structure and validity

Reconstructed, the core argument runs:

1. Percepts, social identities, narratives and world-models are constructions. (Granted.)
2. Every construction yields *content*.
3. "Content is exactly what requires a subject." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-construction-constructor/)
4. "Construction is an operation, and operations require an operator and a field in which they operate." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-construction-constructor/)
5. Iterating construction (a self-model building the sense of self) only adds more content, so it needs a further subject.
6. Therefore some subject is presupposed that is not itself one of the constructions: "The constructor is not itself constructed, on pain of regress." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-construction-constructor/)

**Validity defects.**

- **Equivocation on "operator" (P4).** Read functionally, an operator is the brain or the generative model, and P4 is satisfied by a physical system. Nothing follows about an *experiencer*. Read phenomenally, P4 assumes what it should prove. The regress section moves from "operator" to "subject" without argument: "an operator and a field" becomes "a subject the regress was meant to dissolve" within three sentences. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-construction-constructor/)
- **P3 is false by the article's own lights.** The article grants that lower-level theory of mind "may operate within baseline cognition without phenomenal consciousness." It describes the generative model as maintaining stable priors in a way that, like the thermostat, need involve "nothing it is like." It describes the constructive processes as operating "below introspective access." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-construction-constructor/) All of these are content-bearing representations without a subject. If sub-personal content exists, P3 must be restricted to *conscious* content. The restricted premise is "conscious content requires a conscious subject," which is analytic under phenomenal realism and does no work against a constructionist who claims to construct the conscious subject. This is the article's central internal inconsistency.
- **Regress-stopping fallacy.** "Construction requires an unconstructed constructor" is asserted, not shown. The alternatives are bootstrapping or self-organising loops in which the modelling system and the modelled self co-arise with no unconstructed element. These include Metzinger's PSM, Hofstadter-style strange loops, Friston's self-evidencing, and Laukkonen et al.'s recursive "beautiful loop." [nih](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5945877/)[osf](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/daf5n) The article never states this option, let alone refutes it. "The regress terminates not because we run out of patience but because the terms only make sense relative to a subject" is a semantic claim about the word "content" [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-construction-constructor/) and is question-begging against teleosemantic and representationalist accounts of content.
- **Evidential-independence failure.** The Synthesis table presents four rows as "one problem in four costumes" and claims "recurrence of the same remainder across four independent domains." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-construction-constructor/) Each row's right-hand column is derived from P3. That is not independent support. "Convergence raises the explanatory bar" is coherence inflation, even after the article's own disclaimer.

### (c) Question-begging and the functional-vs-phenomenal constructor

Charges (i) and (ii) both stand.

- **(i) Begging the question.** The four-register descriptions are loaded at the outset. The percept is described as something "undergone"; the social identity as needing "someone *for whom*" it is an identity; the narrative as "experienced"; the world-model as "present" to a "medium-bearer." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-construction-constructor/) Each register builds the experiential subject into its *description of the construction*, then "discovers" it as a presupposition. The inverted-qualia and zombie-community appeals add nothing independent: both are conceivability arguments that presuppose phenomenal realism.
- **(ii) Functional vs phenomenal constructor.** "Something must do the constructing" licenses a constructing system: brain, generative model, PSM-bearing organism. Every rival grants that. The step from *constructing system* to *experiencing subject* is never argued. The article's Evidence section half-sees this: the regress "licenses only the *minimal* claim that some subject is presupposed." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-construction-constructor/) But it never asks whether "subject" in the minimal claim is functional or phenomenal. On the functional reading the minimal claim is uncontested. On the phenomenal reading it is Tenet 1. Calling it "close to forced by the regress" and "independently argued" is the article's most serious calibration error. [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-construction-constructor/)

### (d) Tenet protection and bracketing

- **Tenet leakage.**

- The opacity paragraph claims the dissociation (phenomenal character invariant as functional role changes) "is what dualism predicts and what pure functionalism struggles to accommodate." [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-construction-constructor/) Opacity is itself a functional and representational property in Metzinger's framework, and SMT predicts precisely that opaque representations keep phenomenal character. [nih](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5945877/) This is calibration asymmetry: the datum is credited to dualism while the rival's prediction of it is ignored.

## Part 5 — Site-Wide Methodology Improvements

1. **Status-propagation gate.** Make it mechanical: no apex may assign a claim a higher tier than its source concept or topic page assigns. Here the apex's "cannot escape" outranks the concept page's "internally consistent and not refutable." An automated diff of tier vocabulary between an apex and its listed sources would have caught this.
2. **Confession-to-label conversion rule.** Any sentence of the form "this is a premise X denies rather than overlooks" or "inherited from Tenet N" must trigger a check of the title, abstract, Synthesis and Relation-to-Site-Perspective sections for unconditional restatements. Logged concessions that leave headline wording untouched should fail review.
3. **Mandatory rival roster for self/subject pages.** Every apex in the self cluster should name and engage Metzinger, Dennett, Frankish/illusionism and at least one active-inference account of selfhood (currently Laukkonen, Friston & Chandaria 2025) before Relation to Site Perspective. A missing entry should block publication, as the constrain-vs-establish gate does elsewhere.
4. **Functional/phenomenal disambiguation check.** Flag every occurrence of "subject," "constructor," "operator," "owner" and "for-me" in argumentative premises, and require the author to mark each as functional or phenomenal. Most of this article's force comes from unmarked shifts between the two.
5. **Independence audit for "convergence" claims.** Before any "recurs across independent domains" sentence, require a stated derivation showing that each domain's conclusion does not follow from a shared premise.
6. **Reference lists in apexes.** Apex pages currently lean on source pages for metadata. Require a full external reference list with DOIs and page numbers, so the three-layer citation check can run on the apex itself.
7. **Pipeline-specific risks (AI-assisted generation and Claude-on-Claude review).**

- The target carries a multi-model "AI Generated" byline [unfinishablemap](https://unfinishablemap.org/apex/self-construction-constructor/) and is reviewed by the same model family. The failure modes found here suit same-family review: fluent restatement of a contested premise as a "structural feature," accurate local hedges that never reach headline claims, and silent omission of a rival the reviewer also does not surface.

A same-family reviewer cannot see what neither model raises.
- Countermeasures: (a) seed every self-cluster review with an external, human-curated rival bibliography that the reviewer must check off; (b) route apex-level reviews to a non-Claude model or a human philosopher at least once per major revision; (c) keep the changelog synchronous with page edits. - Human supervision should explicitly sign off on any tier upgrade (e.g. "independently argued") in an apex. These upgrades are exactly what automated review is least likely to challenge.
