## Summary and Verdict

**Verdict: PROCEED.** The subject earns a topics slot because the Map's own register already answers the question the void leaves open, and answers it in the direction that costs most. Saad (2025) shields physics from Boltzmann brains by a *zombie shield*: an extrinsic requirement on consciousness that no fluctuation satisfies. The Map cannot take that shield. [P-CS7](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs7) holds that architectural adequacy suffices for coupling, "provenance included, since an ancestry criterion would install exactly the unexplained selection principle the response was adopted to avoid", and [the built-edge article](/topics/architectural-adequacy-at-the-built-edge/) draws the consequence for Davidson's Swampman: "a complete duplicate takes whatever verdict its original takes ([P-CS7](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs7))". A Boltzmann brain is the cosmological [Swampman](/concepts/swampman/). On the Map's commitments an architecturally adequate fluctuation is a subject, so the Map inherits the full Boltzmann problem with no escape through the theory of consciousness. None of this appears in [observer-selection-void](/voids/observer-selection-void/), which is at 2884 of 3000 words and cannot hold it.

The record lends the tenets nothing. What it shows is that the Map has already made the one move that dissolves the instability face, and made it for an unrelated reason: Tenet 4's refusal to convert the indexical objection into a credence argument is a refusal of Center Indifference, and Elga (2025) shows that without that principle "No threat of undermining or instability is in sight." The price is that the Map may not run Carroll's argument either; it cannot use its own unfluctuated status to constrain cosmology. On the class face the Map is worse placed than externalist physicalism, since it cannot zombify; on the rule and instability faces it is in the same position as Dogramaci and Schoenfield, and for structurally similar reasons. One further finding moves the void directly: Elga's 2024 manuscript, which the void cites for the tentative conclusion that "instability considerations do not provide a satisfying resolution", was published in revised form as Elga (2025, *PPR* 111(1)) with the opposite framing, that the threat of instability "is an illusion". The void's instability face should cite the published paper.

**Saad 2025** (preprint pagination; §§ as in the preprint; print 594–621 not seen):
- §2 [raw]: "the physical theories in question say nothing whatsoever about consciousness and so do not predict that there are any Boltzmann brains, if being a Boltzmann brain definitionally requires being conscious."
- §2.1 [raw]: the skeptical objection runs on phenomenal internalism plus "the self-locating indifference principle".
- §3 [raw]: "Zombified Brains: there is some or other extrinsic physical requirement for consciousness that no Boltzmann brain would satisfy; so no Boltzmann brain is conscious."
- §4 n.19 [raw]: the zombification of Boltzmann creatures "has been advanced as a problem for phenomenal externalist theories—the problem is often put in terms of Davidson's (1987) Swampman." Saad turns the dialectic on its head: the zombifying power is an advantage.
- §5 [raw]: K-max interpretationism, the global shield, "renders each subject's experiences directly sensitive to the entire state of the universe."
- §6 n.26 [raw]: against Schaffer's argument from the global character of laws to physicalism, "local physical differences could be systematically correlated with phenomenal differences as a consequence of a global psychophysical law."
- §7 [raw]: the shield "places a special outcome (the obtaining of a psychophysical principle that suppresses the production of Boltzmann observers and thereby shield physical theories from Boltzmannian objections) within a wide class of unremarkable possible outcomes".
- §8 [raw]: the general psychophysical fine-tuning problem "arises for virtually any view about the realization of experience."
- §9 n.43 [raw]: the multiverse-plus-shield package "construes a psychophysical parameter as invariant" across the ensemble; this is the title's sense of a cosmological parameter.
- §10 [raw]: "since physical theories are not in the business of explaining psychophysical principles, new physics is not apt to account for psychophysical fine-tuning." A multiverse of psychophysical principles fails because "observers are not confined to psychophysically fine-tuned universes"; the surviving explanations are axiarchic meta-law and design.
- §11 [raw]: "avoiding the proliferation of Boltzmann brains should not be unreflectively endorsed as a criterion of adequacy for physical theories."

**Kotzen 2020** [raw, published chapter]:
- p. 26: "Cognitive instability, all by itself, is not a sufficient reason to reject a hypothesis." Instability is "one way in which a hypothesis, when true, is able to 'hide itself' from rational discovery" (dreaming, the Matrix, hypoxia, conspiracy).
- p. 28, against Hartle and Srednicki: "the point of the most plausible versions of epistemic presumptions in favor of typicality is that, if there are two observers at a world who have the same evidence, then I can't have any good reason to believe that I am one of them rather than the other." Their red/blue cycle model commits "a flagrant violation of the Principle of Total Evidence": the strongest statement of the evidence is "that this observing system — i.e., me — exists and observes red."
- p. 31: the disorder argument gives "strong evidence against a cosmology like that — evidence that doesn't depend on the premise that I'm not a Boltzmann Brain", and reasons to accept the Past Hypothesis "are also reasons to reject the hypothesis that I am a Boltzmann Brain."

**Dogramaci 2020** [raw]: the skeptical argument "requires you to make a statistical inference (most Fs are Gs, this is an F, so it's probably a G), but the Principle of Total Evidence blocks us from making the inference". Belief that one is a Boltzmann brain "would have to be entirely based on, and epistemically dependent for its justification on, the ordinary scientific evidence that the universe hosts zillions of BBs." Conclusion: "All of the zillions of BBs should think they are not BBs. I will be right, they will be wrong, and we'll all be rational."

**Dogramaci & Schoenfield 2025** [raw, preprint of July 2024]:
- §1: "rational scientific reasoning is, and is nothing more than, Bayesian updating". Of the two-step skeptical argument, "we take sides with Dogramaci and not Carroll": step 1 (infer a Boltzmann cosmology from evidence) is fine; step 2 (indifference) is the error.
- §2a, the likelihood argument: "The range of possible experiences a BB could have is much greater than the range possible for an OO." So Pr(E|BB) < Pr(E|OO) and the evidence does not raise Pr(BB).
- §3b: Center Indifference, after Builes: "for any two similar centered worlds c1 and c2, if both c1 and c2 are compatible with your evidence, then it is rationally required to set Cr(c1|c1 or c2) = ½." Result 2, that Pr(H_E|E & OO) < ½ for any evidence E, "is an absurd result"; "Cognitive instability, then, is not the culprit." Diagnosis: "my evidence is about me—not about a randomly selected duplicate of me."
- §4, on Bostrom and Chalmers: "indifference reasoning will not make for a plausible or an effective argument for skepticism from empirical evidence."

**Elga 2025** [raw, PPR open access]: "My aim is to clarify the threat of instability and show that it is an illusion." At step one, "Some may doubt that randomly-generated entities are conscious at all" (n.3: "consciousness requires an appropriate evolutionary past", with Saad's bubbles as the rejoinder). Denying the indifference principle "in an extreme way" leaves "No threat of undermining or instability" in sight. The model "implicitly assumes an update rule in the spirit of the 'Self-Sampling Assumption'".

**Carroll 2020** [raw, arXiv preprint]: "Given any reasonable physical criteria for what constitutes a conscious brain, the ratio of BBs to ordinary observers (OOs) in a given cosmological model is almost always going to be very close to either 0 or 1." The standard likelihoods assume that "given some reference class of intelligent observers, we are equally likely to have been any of them, and should reason accordingly".

## Question 1: Does a Dualist Framework Have a Reference Class at All?

The Map's register answers in two halves. **Eligibility is architectural.** [P-CS7](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs7) makes adequacy sufficient and rejects provenance as a criterion, so an adequate fluctuation is coupled. The built-edge article already states the Swampman corollary. This is the exact negation of Saad's Zombified Brains and of Elga's first line of resistance; Saad's remark that zombification "is often put in terms of Davidson's (1987) Swampman" names the Map's commitment from the other side. **Count and pairing are not readable.** [P-I3](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i3) holds that subject boundaries are determinate but "not recoverable from the physical or functional organisation of the system", and [P-SC2](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc2) books the missing pairing law. So the dualist reference class exists as a determinate class of subjects, its membership at the level of *which systems* is fixed by architecture, and its membership at the level of *how many subjects per system* is a latent variable. That is a sharper answer than the void's "the dualist cannot read it off physics": the void's class face should say that on the Map the *eligibility* half is readable and the *census* half is not.

What follows for typicality is the result that matters. Carroll's instability argument needs the indifference likelihoods (his eq. 18); Dogramaci and Schoenfield and Elga (2025) show that the instability dissolves once Center Indifference is denied, and that denying it is what the Map already did in Tenet 4, where "a sentence moving from 'I find myself in one outcome' to 'this is evidence for one world' would need the updating rule no one has". The Map's reason is metaphysical rather than Bayesian: indexical facts are "ontologically real" ([indexical-identity-quantum-measurement](/topics/indexical-identity-quantum-measurement/)), which is the realist version of Dogramaci and Schoenfield's "my evidence is about me—not about a randomly selected duplicate of me" and of Kotzen's "this observing system — i.e., me — exists". The alignment is in the vocabulary, not the evidence; their arguments run on likelihoods and would go through for a deflationist. The consequence is a cost: having forgone typicality, the Map may not use the fact that it is not a fluctuation to select cosmology. Carroll's cosmological use of instability is the same move the Map declines with the indexical objection, and the record here says the decline is consistent only if applied to both.

## Question 2: What Does Saad's Psychophysical Fine-Tuning Cost the Map?

Less than it costs the physicalist who takes the shield, and something different. Saad's fine-tuning is the cost of a psychophysical principle *selected to suppress* Boltzmann observers. The Map's psychophysical law, under [Tenet 1](/tenets/#dualism) and [Tenet 2](/tenets/#minimal-quantum-interaction), is a contingent fundamental law fixing coupling by architecture and influence by outcome-selection; it suppresses nothing, so the shield-specific fine-tuning does not arise. Saad's general problem, which "arises for virtually any view about the realization of experience", does arise: why is the psychophysical law one that couples to ordered rather than disordered systems? The Map's existing fine-tuning discussion ([inheritance-problem-for-the-psychophysical-law](/topics/inheritance-problem-for-the-psychophysical-law/), Brownstein 2025 at [multi-agent-born-preservation-problem](/topics/multi-agent-born-preservation-problem/)) concerns valence and efficacy, not observer production, so this is a new item for the register.

Two of Saad's points bear on the Map's architecture. First, n.26 defends global psychophysical laws against Schaffer's locality argument for physicalism, a defence the Map can use, though the Map's interface is local (a channel to a brain) and K-max interpretationism's input is "the entire state of the universe", so the Map and Saad's globalism are rivals, not allies. Second, §10's finding that new physics "is not apt to account for psychophysical fine-tuning" and that the survivors are meta-law and design is a result the Map should report rather than exploit: under [Tenet 5](/tenets/#occams-limits) the Map holds that fine-tuning reasoning is unreliable with incomplete knowledge, and the honest statement is that it owes an explanation it declines to give by design. The physicalist who takes Saad's shield pays fine-tuning plus globalism plus a multiverse-invariant "psychophysical parameter"; the physicalist who refuses it pays Carroll's cosmological constraint or the Bayesian reply. The Map pays the Bayesian reply, with no shield available.

## Question 3: Does the Pairing-Law Gap Make the Map Worse or Better Placed?

Worse on the class face, by the Map's own standard. A functionalist can fix membership by a functional criterion; an externalist physicalist can fix it by history; the Map fixes eligibility by architecture and then has no rule for count. A Boltzmann brain is a subject whose channel begins and ends within seconds, which is precisely the undated onset and cessation [P-SC3](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc3) registers as unaccounted for. A Boltzmann cosmology turns [P-SC1](/positions/subject-census/#p-sc1)'s census, which the Map holds to be part of the physical state description, into a quantity dominated by fluctuation subjects the Map cannot count. That is the sharpest version of the class face for a dualist, and it is self-inflicted: [P-CS7](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs7) was adopted to avoid an unexplained selection principle, and the Boltzmann case is where the refusal of selection is paid for.

Better on one point. The disorder argument (Kotzen; Dogramaci and Schoenfield's likelihood ratio) requires that what a fluctuation experiences is fixed by its internal state. The Map is internalist about phenomenal content given pairing ([swampman](/concepts/swampman/), [content-externalism](/concepts/content-externalism/)), so the likelihood argument is fully available to it, and it runs without a shield. On [P-CS7](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs7) there is also no fact-free borderline about whether a two-metre bubble is conscious, only an adequacy question with an answer. That is a clarity, not an advantage in evidence.
