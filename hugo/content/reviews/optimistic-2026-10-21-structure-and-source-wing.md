## Executive Summary

Each of the four pages splits its question in two: one half the rival can have, and one it cannot.

- The language-model page gives history the job of fixing *which* referent a word has, and a phenomenal source the job of making something a bearer that can borrow it.
- Conceptual role semantics lets inferential role illuminate "the *structure* of content" without naturalising "its existence".
- The process/content distinction trusts reports of what is experienced over reports of how it is produced.
- The relocation objection grants a theory its correlates and asks only why having them is felt.

Each page then declines the dividend the split seems to offer. The relocation page says its seven worked cases do not "supply seven arguments for dualism". The language-model verdict is "untested rather than confirmed". The process/content page calls itself "calibration craft, not metaphysics". The calibration is exemplary, and the Process Philosopher and the Hardline Empiricist never pull apart.

The openings are seams between pages that should know each other and do not.

- The one proposal that credits LLMs with meaning through conceptual role is missing from the language-model page, though its own research note said to place it. The conceptual-role page has no LLM case at all.
- Nisbett and Wilson's ground-truth design has now been run on language models, and it found a positional bias the models leave out of their explanations. The process/content page does not know this, and the page that reports it names a "Nisbett–Wilson analogue" without linking to it.
- `llm-consciousness` still says LLM symbols "lack genuine meaning", which the Map stopped holding on 10-11.
- The relocation page's own diagnostic has a worked instance for content on the conceptual-role page. Neither page links the other.

All four repairs together cost 267 words, 63 of them reference apparatus.

There are two genuine gaps:

- **Singular thought.** Does thinking about a particular require acquainted presentation? It has zero live coverage, and it is the question underneath the language-model page's hardest case.
- **Ericsson and Simon's protocol analysis.** It is the main methodological rival to Nisbett–Wilson, and it also has zero coverage.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Chalmers would find himself quoted fairly twice. The relocation page states his hard problem in a form "every camp recognises" (L35). It then takes the Chalmers–Jackson 2001 reply as the Map's and marks exactly where it is disputed: "The dispute between the Map and these rivals lies at that premise, and neither side has closed it" (L85). On the language-model page, his 2023 pure thinkers appear with his own stipulation intact: "pure thinkers would be conscious and could undergo nonsensory experiences" (L79). The page draws the right moral. His argument removes the *sensory*-grounding objection, and "the Map's objection was never sensory" (L81). He would also note the courtesy that "Both disclaimers are explicit, so neither is an error". The page cites his paper for what it shows and does not recruit it for what he set aside.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Stapp has the least to praise here, and the pages are right to give him little. The relocation page keeps the interface out of the objection's protection: "The objection does not protect the interface commitments, where the Map's empirical exposure lies" (L119). The process/content page says introspection cannot reach "the post-decoherence-selection interface". The content of an intention can be reported, but "the content-to-physics mechanism cannot be introspected" (L87). Stapp would welcome the honesty about where the risk sits: in physics, not in introspection. The language-model page states the dependence he would want stated: "the verdict for *current* LLMs is only as secure as that programme" (L125).

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

Nagel's fixed point is the relocation page's step 3: "'Pain as felt' is not obviously exhausted by any role, so filling the role leaves a remainder" (L53). The process/content page is his method page. It keeps reports of *what experience is like* on the table after Nisbett–Wilson, because those reports are "content claims, which the Nisbett-Wilson critique does not target" (L81). The language-model page's Tenet 4 paragraph would interest him most. An LLM's "I" inherits no single author's referent, and "With no subject of its own, if [P-AC1](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac1) holds, it has no non-derivative referent" (L123). On this account the first person is where derived reference strains hardest.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Whitehead would praise the conceptual-role page's horn (a). Norms of correct inference form "a second irreducible register alongside phenomenal character" (L57), and "The space of reasons, the Map holds, is real and irreducible" (L61). Irreducible normativity is congenial to a metaphysics of value-laden process. The page also keeps Sellars at arm's length: it is "that final reductive step the Map resists". None of the four pages uses process-philosophical resonance to move anything up the evidential scale. The language-model page's Swampman cell rests on [P-CS7](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs7) "held at moderate credence" (L94) and does not inflate it.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Kane would want more from the process/content page's Tenet 3 paragraph and would find it correct as it stands: "The felt sense of deciding is a content report and can stand as evidence that there is something it is like to act intentionally" (L83). The story of *how* deciding caused doing is a process report, and it inherits the confabulation void. The page claims the phenomenology of agency without claiming that introspection shows its efficacy. That is the honest form of Tenet 3, fitting the tenet without evidencing it. The language-model page applies the same discipline to authorship: "derived reference locates every original referential act upstream, in conscious authors" (L125). It marks that link as weak.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

McGinn appears by name on the process/content page, and the page is neutral on his claim. It "documents the empirical asymmetry without committing to whether the asymmetry could in principle be reduced" (L96), and it notes that microphenomenological gains suggest "*some* of the asymmetry is contingent". The relocation page shows the same humility towards illusionism: "Illusionism has not yet delivered, and the Map has not shown that it cannot" (L83). It applies the same tractability test to the Map's own narrowing (L101). The language-model page leaves its hardest case open: "The Map holds the first as a proposal and records the fourth as the price if it fails" (L111).

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

None of the four pages touches minimal-organism consciousness, so the five-tier scale arrives here only as a methodology. It is used well.

- **Process/content distinction.** The core Nisbett–Wilson finding is rated "*strongly supported*". The page adds at once that "The layered-trust framing the Map draws from the distinction is a consequence, not the finding itself" (L39). - **Conceptual role semantics.** It keeps an intra-naturalist objection from being cashed as a dualist win: holism "is a standing cost of CRS, not a refutation" (L49).
