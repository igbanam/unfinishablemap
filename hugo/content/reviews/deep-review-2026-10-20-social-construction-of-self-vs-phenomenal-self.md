## Pessimistic Analysis Summary

### Critical Issues Found

1. **Rochat: the direction of the neonate finding was inverted (empirical fidelity).** The text read "preferential response to self-touch". The primary study reports the opposite. Rochat & Hespos 1997 (OpenAlex abstract): "Newborns tended to display significantly more rooting responses ... following external compared to self-stimulation". Rochat 2003 (PDF, grepped, p. 722) says: "neonates do root significantly more to external compared to self-stimulation ... From birth, infants differentiate between self- vs. non-self touch". **Fix**: changed to "rooting less to their own touch than to an experimenter's". The list item "basic affective states" is not something Rochat 2003 gives as Level-1 self-specification. His only neonatal emotion item is *imitation* of basic emotions (Field et al. 1982), which is a social interaction and so would undercut "pre-social". Replaced it with Rochat's own "visual-proprioceptive" self-specifying experience (p. 723). The 07-06 guard caveats (biological self-model compatibility, the Rochat staging caveat, under-determination) are untouched.

2. **Winawer: the comparison was misdescribed and the conclusion overstated.** The text read "discriminate between these shades faster than English speakers—a demonstration that linguistic categories shape perceptual experience". The abstract (OpenAlex, verbatim) reports something different. Russian speakers were faster when two blues fell in *different* Russian categories than when they fell in the same one. English speakers "did not show a category advantage". A verbal (not spatial) dual task eliminated the advantage. The authors conclude that categories "affect performance on simple perceptual color tasks" and that "the effect of language is online". **Fix**: the sentence now describes the within-language category advantage, the English null and the verbal-interference abolition, and concludes "shape perceptual discrimination online". The sibling sentence in Cross-cultural invariance changed from "carve experience differently across cultures" to "carve colour discrimination differently across languages". The sibling research note `research/voids-categorical-perception-void-2026-09-15` L84 already had the correct description.

3. **Internal inconsistency in how the article routes its priority claim (calibration).** The 07-06 fixes changed the two empirical planks but left the closing developmental paragraph of "The Dependence Runs One Way" in its old form. It still said: "This is not merely a thought experiment ... Phenomenal selfhood seems to precede social selfhood in ontogeny. The neonate has phenomenal experience before social self-construction begins." That flatly asserts the claim which the Developmental-priority plank says the developmental evidence "under-determines". The dissolution paragraph also put the priority weight on the "developmental (neonate) and phylogenetic arguments" alone, while the neonate plank puts the phenomenal-subject claim on "the transcendental argument". A reviewer who accepts the tenets would still flag this. **Fix**: the closing paragraph now says the developmental evidence "is consistent with the asymmetry: a self-specifying system is present in the neonate before social self-construction begins, though, as noted above, the data leave open whether it is yet a phenomenal subject". This also removes the "This is not merely X" construct. The dissolution paragraph now routes the weight to "the transcendental argument and the developmental (neonate) and phylogenetic evidence below". The forward reference in the neonate plank now has a named anchor: `[[#the-dependence-runs-one-way|transcendental argument developed below]]`. The 07-06 guard (dissolution is neutral; the weight lies elsewhere) is kept.

### Medium Issues Found

- **Schachter–Singer symptom list was embellished.** The text read "elevated heart rate, flushed face, focused attention". Focused attention is not an epinephrine symptom in the 1962 paper. The PDF (uniba.it copy, grepped) lists "palpitation, tremor, and sometimes a feeling of flushing and accelerated breathing". The informed condition was told "your hand will start to shake, your heart will start to pound, and your face may get warm and flushed". **Fix**: changed to "pounding heart, trembling hands, flushed face". The euphoria and anger conditions were confirmed in the text (44 and 46 hits).
- **Dreamless sleep was stated flatly as cessation of phenomenal consciousness.** "when phenomenal consciousness ceases (dreamless sleep, anaesthesia)" conflicts with [sleep-and-consciousness](/concepts/sleep-and-consciousness/). That page's table gives NREM3 "phenomenal status contested" and its L118 says dreamless sleep "cannot be enlisted". It also conflicts with the witness-consciousness traditions this same paragraph cites. **Fix**: changed to "if phenomenal consciousness ever ceases (dreamless sleep and anaesthesia are the [contested](/concepts/sleep-and-consciousness/) candidates)".
- **Gallagher 2000 title was truncated.** Crossref gives "Philosophical conceptions of the self: implications for cognitive science". Subtitle added.
