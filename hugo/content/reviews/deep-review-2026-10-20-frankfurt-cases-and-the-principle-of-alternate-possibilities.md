### Critical Issues Found

- **C1. Internal inconsistency on the scope of "source incompatibilism"** (Leeway or Source? vs Relation to Site Perspective). The pivot section said Frankfurt's success leaves "source incompatibilism untouched", and that on "the source view" an agent could lack alternatives and still be responsible. The Relation section, after the 07-09 refine, says the Map's own *wide* source position (sourcehood carries alternatives) is *not* left untouched, since triggering the fallback relocates the Map to *narrow* source. Both claims hold only of the narrow form. **Fixed**: "On the source view in its *narrow* form (a *wide* form is explained below)…" and "They leave *narrow* source incompatibilism untouched". This finishes the July wide/narrow repair, which the refine applied to the Relation section only.
- **C2. Dropped qualifier in the Vihvelin Preemptor gloss.** "Leaves the agent's intrinsic properties, and with them her abilities, untouched" made the abilities follow from intrinsic properties alone. On Vihvelin's account only *narrow* abilities do. Wide ability survives only through a second step, her claim that the Preemptor is no obstacle, and that is the step Franklin attacks ("isn't this an obstacle par excellence?"). **Fixed**: "leaves the agent's intrinsic properties untouched and, by Vihvelin's definition of an obstacle, never stands in the way of the abilities they ground". This avoids a "her" that could refer to Vihvelin (the 10-19 fix) and keeps "narrow" from colliding with "narrow source".
