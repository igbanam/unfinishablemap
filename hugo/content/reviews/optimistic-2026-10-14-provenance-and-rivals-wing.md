## Executive Summary

The four pages share one virtue, and it is the one the Map's evidential-status discipline is written to produce: each takes an ally or a rival at full strength and then declines the easy use of it. The Popper article treats the most prominent twentieth-century philosophical ally of the Map's interactionism as "provenance without inheritance": it takes his open causal closure and his conservation reply, refuses his World-3 route to mind because the Map has made no case for a third causal realm, and quotes his verdict on the Map's own mechanism family without softening it ("I still do not love the baby"). The structuralism article lets the rival name itself, grades it in three strengths, concedes the weakest, borrows the critics' arguments while recording that none of them is a dualist ally, and closes "argued, not won". The assent article says in its lede that "The record carries no evidence for the Map's tenets" and in its tenet section that the history "removes an objection to interaction and supplies no evidence for it". The apex's 21:51 pass did the same work from the other side: a 2026-04-21 synthesis that had let empirical tractability "tilt the scales" now says such data "could separate dualism from physicalism, not from process monism".

The opportunities are structural. (1) The page the Map calls "the most direct rival to the Dualism tenet" has four inbound links, and none of them comes from the pages that assert what it denies: `inverted-qualia`, `reductionism`, `phenomenal-acquaintance`, `knowledge-argument` and the process apex all state the structural/non-structural distinction without pointing at the rival, and `consciousness-and-the-problem-of-measurement-standards` discusses the rival's flagship experiment and its "creed" without the link. (2) The assent article ends where its own last sentence says a second history begins ("The objections of Hobbes and Spinoza belong to a later history"); the corpus has Spinoza only as Gilbert's label, Newman's *Grammar of Assent* nowhere live, and James's "Will to Believe" only on pragmatism pages. (3) The Popper article's one unresolved sentence ("pending a check") names two texts nobody on the Map has read, and its sharpest surviving complaint, the grain complaint, has a Map answer-shape on `temporal-consciousness-structure-and-agency` that neither page links. (4) The apex twice says a framework "strongly supports" a tenet where what it has shown is compatibility plus generalisation; the Process Philosopher's praise is being cashed out in the Hardline Empiricist's vocabulary, which is the one slippage this review format exists to catch.

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)
The structuralism article locates the dispute exactly where a property dualist would put it, at individuation versus constitution, and does so in the proponents' own words: Fink, Kob and Lyre concede that "location within a structure may uniquely identify an experience, but not that this makes an experience what it is" and leave scientists "agnostic" on the rest. The Map reads that as its own form-and-content argument in another vocabulary and does not pretend the similarity data decide it either way. Lyre's concession about quiddities, "As a metaphysical possibility, I fear, this cannot be ruled out ... If this is the core difficulty about qualia, then the hard problem cannot be solved structurally", is quoted with its scope and then with Lyre's own argument that the scope is empty, so the reader sees the disagreement narrow to whether a theory may set that possibility aside. The Popper article keeps the Map's Dualism tenet argued from the explanatory gap, apart from Bidirectional Interaction, precisely because Gray's sentence ("to show a causal connection is not to mark an ontological distinction") would otherwise apply to the Map as it applies to Popper. That is the hard problem doing its proper work as the Map's ground, with causal efficacy kept out of the argument for irreducibility.

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)
The Popper page is a hostile-witness section done right. After the 21:26 correction it no longer claims the Map's selector escaped Popper by novelty of mechanism; it quotes Popper's own description of minds working "by influencing or selecting some quantum jumps", his verdict on the refinement "just within the Heisenberg uncertainties" ("perhaps a slight advance ... But I still do not love the baby"), and names the two complaints that survive, Schlick's and the grain complaint. The Map's guided indeterminism is then stated as "structurally a candidate for Popper's intermediate category" whose candidacy "has to be earned against both complaints". The apex, for its part, keeps Stapp's Heisenberg and Dirac choices as the most developed attempt to integrate process thought with quantum mechanics while recording that critics "question whether Stapp's interpretation of quantum mechanics is standard", and its new L103 says what interface data could and could not show. A quantum-mind theorist should prefer this to the April version, because the claim that survives is the one that can be defended.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)
The assent article's best material is first-personal and six centuries old. Holcot's psychological argument is an introspective report: every man "experiences in himself (*experitur in seipso*) that he cannot by command of the will determine himself to assent to one side of a contradiction, when both are open to question for him". The article places it against the exact case Aquinas reserved for the will and shows that the modern introspective route to involuntarism (Alston's test) has a medieval form, without claiming they are the same argument. Its own synthesis, the Stoic double claim read through Goulet-Cazé's *eixis*, gives one act two phenomenological faces, "the passivity of a ruling part 'which lets itself be swept along by a representation'" and the same act "once subject to the control of reason", which is the structure the assent void calls agency without a handle. The structuralism article contributes Negro's Relational Mary, a knowledge-argument variant in which character outruns relations, and Lyre's admission that quiddities "certainly cannot be captured by any structuralist conception".

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)
Popper's dualism is an event dualism. "Like Descartes, I propose the adoption of a dualistic outlook, though I do not of course recommend talking of two kinds of interacting substances. But I think it is helpful and legitimate to distinguish two kinds of interacting *states* (or events)". The Popper article quotes this in full, and the apex opens its Process Turn with the same inversion ("The fundamental units are not substances but events"). Neither page notices the other, which is the one cross-wing observation this persona can add (Priority 3b): the Map's most prominent interactionist ally stands nearer the process ontology the apex draws on than to the Cartesian substance picture, and nearer than the Map's own agency cluster, which leans toward a persisting subject. On the apex itself, Whitehead's denial of "vacuous actuality" is presented as Whitehead's panpsychism and set against the Map's interactionism in the same paragraph; the 21:51 pass added that interface data cannot separate dualism from process monism, which is the concession a process philosopher is owed. **Constraint check**: no organism or system is graded on the five-tier scale anywhere in these four pages, so this persona's praise has nothing to upgrade. The one place its praise could be cashed as evidence is the apex's "strongly supports" at L115 and L129, and that is handed to the Hardline Empiricist below rather than praised here.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)
Popper is quoted saying what a libertarian wants said and what a libertarian must answer. What rational behaviour needs is "something intermediate in character between perfect chance and perfect determinism", and the thesis that nothing intermediate can exist is "clearly absurd"; but a single amplified quantum jump "has no similarity to any rational decision", and considered decisions are "reached by a kind of maturing process which is not well represented by the master-switch model". The article makes the Map's Bidirectional Interaction tenet a candidate for Popper's intermediate category and then says the grain complaint is "untouched" by the Map's luck-objection reply: "if decisions mature over lengthy deliberation, a mechanism located at single quantum events owes an account of how many selections add up to one choice". That sentence is the libertarian's real debt stated by a libertarian-friendly page, and the Map has a page where the answer-shape lives (Priority 3a). The assent article is equally candid about Chrysippus: the cylinder "is an early compatibilism about assent" and "a rival's ancestor, not an ally". The apex's Bergson paragraph (the free act "emerges from the whole self") is the phenomenological statement of the same grain point, and its ~280 ms commitment point is now attributed to Thura and Cisek's reach decisions in monkeys rather than left as a bare number.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)
Three pages say what they have not read. The Popper article says in its lead and twice in its References that *The Self and Its Brain* "was not read for this article", that Feyerabend is "reported by Gray", and that the 1977 *Dialectica* paper is "where to look" for whether Popper's anti-epiphenomenalist arguments stand free of World 3. The assent article says it is "reconstructed from translations and encyclopedia scholarship", that Holcot is "known here only through the translations" in one SEP entry, and that whether Stoic assent was "voluntary" in any modern sense "is disputed". The structuralism article marks Aldé, Loorits 2026 and Nimmo "abstract only here" and declines to let an abstract revise a 2014 attribution. The apex keeps its testability note: process philosophy's core claims "don't generate distinctive empirical predictions", and the same is said of substance metaphysics. These are the disclosures that make the Priority List's research seeds precise rather than vague.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)
This persona's verdict is favourable on the three articles and qualified on the apex.

The assent article is the cleanest case of the day. The lede: "The record carries no evidence for the Map's tenets." The tenet section: the convergence of Stoics, Augustine, Aquinas and Holcot on a limit to believing at will "removes an objection to interaction and supplies no evidence for it", and "Dualism gains nothing from the record". Where the article's reading departs from its cited scholarship (Boespflug counts the same authors as direct voluntarists) the 16:40 pass made the disagreement visible at the point of divergence instead of letting the reader infer agreement. No five-tier claim is made; the method/history transitions (origin to validity, past exclusion to present residue) are not attempted.

The structuralism article makes no evidential-tier claim and keeps its calibration vocabulary consistent from lede to close: "argued, not won"; "grounds rather than a verdict"; "unrefuted rather than confirmed". Its two live countermodels (Loorits's debunking of introspective simplicity; Nimmo's no-further-first-person-fact) are named as unrefuted, and the Map's haecceity commitment is stated as something "the Map owes an argument" for. That is the correct register and the deep review's stability note is right that it must not be upgraded on tenet-coherence alone.

The Popper article applies the evidential-status discipline to an ally by name: "his eminence adds no evidential weight". It also applies it against itself: the Minimal Quantum Interaction line says the penny-toss objection "names the burden the Map's selection posit has to carry, and the Map has not yet discharged it".

The apex is where this persona has work. The 21:51 pass is this persona's own kind of edit (Warren's dispute installed; the epothilone result given its classical reading; "Neither has been independently replicated"; "tilts the scales" removed). Two residues remain, both pre-dating today. L115: "Process philosophy strongly supports this claim—indeed, it generalizes it." L129: "Whitehead developed process philosophy before many-worlds was proposed, yet his framework strongly supports this rejection." In both the support offered is compatibility plus generalisation (every occasion has subjective aim; every occasion achieves definite form), which is tenet-coherent, not evidence-elevating; "strongly supports" is the evidential adverb for a compatibility result. The Process Philosopher's praise and this persona's praise diverge on exactly these two sentences, which under this review's own rule makes them a calibration concern for a `refine-draft`, not a strength (Priority 4). Elsewhere the apex is calibrated: L93 "The parallel is structural, not mechanistic"; L109 "On the Map's reading" (installed today); L151 the testability note.
