## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Chalmers would single out the comic's use of Clark's three conditions. The structural theory fixes the *object* of humour and leaves the felt response as a separate term: "Incongruity theory characterizes the **object** of humor with precision and leaves the **felt amusement** as an extra term" (`the-comic` L61). This is the explanatory gap found inside a rival theory's own definition, not imposed on it from outside.

He would also approve the hub's formal-precision passage. It is still the wing's sharpest single argument, and it still refuses the easy case. "More structural detail specifies the gap more precisely rather than closing it" (L118).

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

Stapp gets little here, and correctly so. The hub's Minimal Quantum Interaction paragraph ends "The connection remains speculative, and the phenomenological arguments stand independently" (L158). The void's femtosecond paragraph is labelled "a speculative possibility" (L179). Stapp would most welcome a passage the wing did not write for him: `mirth-void` L94 says the interface is "narrow and selective rather than a general remote control". It then refuses to count the mirth data as evidence for that picture, calling it "compatible with … not evidence for it". That is the honest scale for a selection-only interface.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The music article remains the wing's most Nagelian page. Its question survives everything said below about its missing rival: "When a cello plays a slow, descending phrase in a low register, most listeners experience something that resembles grief. But grief *about what*?" (L99). `mirth-void` adds a Nagelian discipline the older pages lack. It locates the "what it is like" residue and then declines to take it up: "what mirth *feels like* beyond its structure. That residue is the subject of the sibling article and is left there" (L70).

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

Whitehead would read the music article's Bergsonian passage as process philosophy in miniature. "C heard after G in the context of a C major phrase is qualitatively different from C heard after F in the context of an F major phrase" (L73). The note is constituted by its inheritance.

He would like still more the relational ontology that `consciousness-and-the-phenomenology-of-place` borrows from Böhme. Atmosphere is "the common reality of the perceiver and the perceived" (place L72; the quote was publisher-verified in that article's 2026-10-08 ledger). That is the third option `aesthetics-beyond-art` reaches for at L115, without the verbal-dispute deflation.

No organism or system is up for a tier move in this wing, so the evidential-status constraint on this persona is not engaged. The relational reading earns praise only as a better *statement* of an open option, not as support for any reading.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Kane's article is the hub's creation section. The register now agrees it is the one premise standing apart ([P-MC4](/positions/arguments-for-mental-causation/#p-mc4)), and the hub says so in its own words: "Only aesthetic creation is separable—a causal argument that survives both a closed gap and a successful holistic reduction, though its zombie-artist sharpening borrows the conceivability intuition back" (L108, link markup stripped).

He would also respect `mirth-void`'s will gap. Deliberate laughter runs on a different pathway, a "voluntary" system that "originates in the premotor/frontal opercular areas" (L62, quoting Wild et al. 2003). An agent who cannot command her own amusement is still an agent. The void does not turn that limit into an argument either way.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

`aesthetic-testimony` L92 is the wing's best mysterian sentence, and it is symmetric:

> "a reluctance that survives every relocation of its supposed ground is as good a candidate for a phenomenon with no location as for a joint in nature the theories keep missing. Nothing about the invariance itself decides between those readings."

McGinn would add that this is what cognitive closure looks like from inside a debate. The theories keep moving the barrier and the felt phenomenon does not move.
