## Executive Summary

The two pages are among the most carefully calibrated in the dualism cluster. The articulability page treats an open problem as an object of study: "A coherent stipulation is still a stipulation" (L88); "An unpaid bill is a cost, not a disproof" (L94). It also lists what would *not* count as progress (L121), which is rare. The Q3 page finds the cell's instability and gives it a mechanism: "The slide is not a mistake the philosopher makes; it is the position telling the truth about itself" (L64). Its best idea is a pole that is "thin in *thickness*" yet "thick in *explanatory indispensability*" (L76).

Read together, they turn out to be two halves of one problem ([The Convergence](#the-convergence)). When the articulability page closes Q1's gap from the mind side, the mind-only inflation lands in **Q3**, which is exactly the cell whose dualist occupancy the Q3 page shows to be unstable. And the Q3 page's stabilisers presuppose the specified channel that the articulability page says is unpaid (Q3 page L78). Neither page states the loop, and both pages and their siblings get its geometry wrong in four places:

- **Mind-only inflation is routed to Q4.** The taxonomy defines Q3 as max-mind / min-physical and files "Myers–James subliminal-mind views combined with lean physics" there (L107, L113). `interactionist-dualism` L175 and the Q3 page L92 agree. Yet articulability L72 says a mind-side ground "inflates the mind side toward Q4", taxonomy L159 says "toward Q4 (if it requires thick mind)", and both `mechanism-costs` L130 and `dualism-cartography` L125 put "Myers–James subliminal selves" under drift *toward Q4*. `mechanism-costs` contradicts itself: its own L97 lists the same Myers–James views as Q3 inhabitants. The slip comes from the Q4 slogan ("two rich realms, genuinely distinct, genuinely interacting"), which invites writers to count a working physical pole as a thick one. The taxonomy's `#limits-thickness` (L79) says that is the measure the grid does not track.
- **Goff is still filed in Q4 by two stale siblings.** The 2026-08-03 taxonomy refine (`681f58127e`, "misfiles … Goff's cosmopsychism") moved priority cosmopsychism to the Q3 monist limit (L114). `mechanism-costs` L109 and `dualism-cartography` L71 still list it among Q4 inhabitants.
- **MQI's reach is stated three ways.** Taxonomy L149 and cartography L121 say MQI "does not directly constrain mind-side thickness". The Q3 page L76 says MQI keeps the *channel* narrow. Articulability L131 says the Map "cannot pay a psychophysical selection debt by inflating either pole — the obvious closure moves are precisely what MQI forecloses". The first two agree, and the third conflates channel width with mind-side richness, a conflation the taxonomy's Stapp paragraph (L139) warns against.
- **The Q3 page never mentions Q4.** Its title names a "Q3–Q4" boundary that the body never discusses. The name came from the 05-19d review, and nothing in the article explains it. The fix in item 3(c) makes the article earn its title from material it already has.

One calibration concern surfaced too, in the Process Philosopher / Hardline Empiricist tension. The Q3 page says its analysis "explains the *mechanism* by which the other tenets enforce that ruling rather than leaving it as a bare stipulation" (Q3 page L92). But its first stabiliser is functional: physics "supplies the menu". The Map's own framing page concedes that Kastrup's universal consciousness "plays the functional role that physical law plays in dualism" (`analytic-idealism` L54), and that the Map's preference for dualism over idealism is "underdetermined by the data" (`analytic-idealism` L92). Function alone cannot separate the Map's menu-supplier from Kastrup's. What separates them is the Dualism tenet's claim that the supplier exists distinctly from consciousness, so the ruling stays a tenet commitment.

## The Convergence

### One geometry, two halves

1. **The exit lands in Q3.** Articulability L72 names the two ways to close the fiction-to-nature gap: grounding authority in "richer phenomenal facts" or in "richer physical structure". By the taxonomy's own cell definitions (L85, L96, L107, L119), the first leaves Q1 (min/min) for Q3 (max-mind/min-physical) and the second for Q2. The page's "degenerate boundary between Q2 and Q4" should therefore read "Q2 and Q3". The mind-side corner Q1 would collapse into is then the cell where, on the Q3 page's analysis, a dualist position "tend[s] to drift across its lower edge into monism" (L28).
2. **The stabilisers presuppose the bill.** The Q3 page's stabilisers are a selection-only channel and bidirectional symmetry. They hold only "whether its interface can be specified non-arbitrarily — the open question the companion taxonomy assigns as Q1's articulability burden" (L78). That is the Map's *control law*, which articulability L92 says "is unspecified in the same way".
3. **The loop.** Expanding the mind side is the move that could pay the Map's control-law debt, because a phenomenal ground for the law is an intrinsic-feature ground. That move leads into Q3, and the Q3 page's protection there depends on the debt being paid. Stated as one sentence: *the Map's room to expand along the mind axis is room toward Q3, its anti-slide guarantees are conditional on the same law Q1 has not articulated, and the mind-side ground is the one exit MQI leaves open.* Neither page says this. Each would be more exact for saying it, and items 1 and 3 install it at about +80 words in total.

### Both pages find the label slipping

The Q3 page's diagnosis is that, once the mind does enough work, "calling the residue 'physical and real' becomes a label without a referent" (L64). The analytic-idealism page asks the mirror question of Kastrup: his mind-at-large "plays the functional role that physical law plays in dualism. The question becomes whether calling it 'mental' rather than 'physical' is a substantive claim or a relabelling" (L54). The two pages reach the same point from opposite sides. At the Q3 boundary, function does not fix the label. This strengthens the Q3 page's diagnosis. It also explains why its stabilisers cannot do the further work L92 assigns them (item 3a).

## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Articulability L62 names the "plausible minimum" for a ground as a psychophysical law "of the same modal standing as a law of nature, discovered rather than declared". It then concedes that the tenets "*posit* that such a law operates; positing it is not articulating it". Chalmers's own framework is used here as a criterion, not as a comfort. The Q3 page's phrase "a different debt with a different shape" (L38) treats idealism's recovery debt as the same kind of thing as the dualist's interface debt. That fits Chalmers's 2019 paper "Idealism and the Mind-Body Problem", which the Map has not yet engaged (see [Expansion Opportunities](#expansion-opportunities)).

### The Quantum Mind Theorist (Stapp)

"The mind chooses; physics constrains the choice set" (Q3 page L72). Stapp would recognise the division of labour, in which the dynamics fixes the possibilities and the agent's act operates among them. He would also mark a difference the page could note in a later pass. On Stapp's account the agent chooses the *question* (process 1) and nature picks the answer, whereas the Map's channel biases the *outcome*. That difference does not touch the stabiliser argument, which needs only that physics supplies the menu.

### The Phenomenologist (Nagel)

The transparency problem asks "why does the intrinsic mental reality appear, from the outside, *exactly* as impersonal lawful physical structure?" (Q3 page L38). That is Nagel's inner/outer question turned back on the idealist. The most recent book-length idealism answers it in Nagel's own terms. Yetter-Chappell's *The View from Everywhere* (OUP 2025) is a deliberate echo of *The View from Nowhere*, and it has a chapter on "Science, Structure, and Spacetime". The Map names this problem on five pages and states no idealist's answer to it.

### The Process Philosopher (Whitehead)

The Q3 page's treatment of Whitehead is generous and exact: "Whitehead may be best read as having dissolved the slide by refusing the two-substance framing from the start" (L50). Its own stabilised position avoids crude dualism in the same spirit. The physical pole is not a second rich substance but one that does indispensable work. As the next persona notes, that praise has to stay on the coherence axis.

### The Libertarian Free Will Defender (Kane)

Articulability L129: Bidirectional Interaction "forbids the cheap escape" into epiphenomenalism, "so the law must genuinely be paid". L133 adds that without No Many Worlds "the authority question dissolves into indexical illusion". Kane's point that a real choice needs one real outcome is built into why the problem is a problem at all.

### The Mysterian (McGinn)

"It is not obvious what *kind* of natural fact could play the role an agent's stipulation plays in the fictional cases" (articulability L70). The progress criteria count a well-grounded *impossibility* result as "progress of the opposite sign" (L118). Of the pages reviewed, this one comes closest to McGinn's humility without letting it become defeatism.

### The Hardline Empiricist (Birch)

The verdict on these two pages is mixed. The articulability page is a model of restraint. It refuses to read Saad's coherence as discharge (L88), and it applies Occam's limits in both directions (L135): "forbids treating Q1's cheapness as *evidence* that Q1 is correct, and equally forbids treating the unpaid bill as *evidence* that Q1 is false". The Q3 page separates its registers explicitly, with historical accuracy at L42 and methodological position at L68, and it says the stabilisers show "what the Map's commitments *would* secure, not that occupancy has been demonstrated" (L94).

The tension with the Process Philosopher comes at one sentence. Q3 page L92 says the analysis makes the Dualism tenet's exclusion of idealism a matter of mechanism "rather than leaving it as a bare stipulation". The stabilisers are functional. The Map's framing page concedes that idealism's structure plays the same functional role (`analytic-idealism` L54) and that the dualism/idealism choice is "underdetermined by the data" (`analytic-idealism` L92). So the Q3 page's L92 uses tenet-coherence to do discriminating work. That is the pattern this persona names: *tenet-coherent, not evidence-elevating*. The fix (item 3a) keeps the stabilisers as conditions and returns the discrimination to the tenet, matching the analytic-idealism page's calibration. Item 3(b) is related. Bidirectional symmetry is offered as an independent second stabiliser, but analytic idealism already reads influence in both directions as "one portion of consciousness affect[ing] another" (`analytic-idealism` L108). The second stabiliser therefore depends on the first.
