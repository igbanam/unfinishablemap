**Redundancy Hunter**
- `machine-question` No-Many-Worlds paragraph: "therefore", "thus not ... at all" and "load-bearing question" were stacked intensifiers. All were cut, saving words that offset the L100 fix.

**Narrative Flow Analyst**
- `what-it-might-be-like-to-be-an-ai` No-Many-Worlds ended on "may lack the foundation on which all other dimensions of consciousness rest". That is the exact inference both sibling syntheses (`machine-question`, `open-question-ai-consciousness`) name as tempting and then reverse. The three AI syntheses therefore disagreed with one another, and with [P-I4](/positions/individuation-and-subjecthood/#p-i4), [P-AC3](/positions/ai-consciousness-scope/#p-ac3) and now [P-CS7](/positions/consciousness-scope/#p-cs7).
