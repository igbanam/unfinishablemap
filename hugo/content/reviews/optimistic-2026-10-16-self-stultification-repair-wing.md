## Praise from Sympathetic Philosophers

### The Property Dualist (Chalmers)

Chalmers would recognise the rewritten Coincidence Response as fair to the position he spent a chapter of *The Conscious Mind* defending against this very charge.

- **The old text** said the correlation "would be, at best, a fortunate coincidence maintained by pre-established harmony between two causally disconnected domains".
- **The new text** says "The epiphenomenalist need not call the correlation luck", then names the debt: "What this reply still owes is an account of how a lawful parallel yields *knowledge* of experience without causal traffic from it."

The explanatory gap stays a gap about knowledge, where he locates it, rather than being turned into a charge of improbability. The Yetter-Chappell section is the strongest piece of property-dualist engagement on the Map. It states her move in her words ("emerges from inconsistently combining (epiphenomenalist) dualism about qualia with a physicalistic conception of subjects of experience"), notes that "The paper defends epiphenomenalism; it places no comparable constraint on interactionism", and concedes "a real retreat from the classic conclusion, worth conceding plainly".

What Chalmers would miss is his own reply. His naturalistic-dualist answer to the paradox of phenomenal judgment, on which phenomenal belief is justified by acquaintance and partly constituted by the experience rather than caused by it, is the most-cited dualist reply to this argument. It appears on no page in the wing. See High-Priority opportunity A.
